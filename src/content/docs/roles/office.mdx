## Who this is for

This page suits people whose work moves between documents and people, such as general affairs, HR, finance, planning, operations, and team managers.

- After every meeting, writing up the notes and follow-up tasks becomes another job.
- Writing reports and emails takes more time than you'd expect.
- Cleaning up and summarizing Excel data is always about the same, yet it takes a lot of manual effort.

## Top 5 ways AI helps

### 1. Organize meeting notes and pull out to-dos

**What it can do**: Extract decisions, owners, and deadlines into a table from your notes or a transcript.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. For recordings, first turn them into text with [Transcribe and organize meeting and lecture recordings](/media/14-transcribe-recordings/). Summarizing tips are in [Summarize meeting notes and long documents and pull out to-dos](/chat/02-summarize-documents/).

<PromptBox label="Organize meeting notes">
Below are my meeting notes. Organize them in this format.
1) The meeting's purpose in one line
2) Decisions (numbered list)
3) Action items table: task, owner, deadline, notes
4) Open issues not yet decided
If an owner or deadline isn't in the notes, write "TBD" and don't make one up.

[Paste meeting notes]
</PromptBox>

### 2. Draft reports and plans

**What it can do**: Set up an outline, write a draft for each section, and adjust the tone for the reader.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. Follow [Write and polish report and proposal drafts](/chat/04-write-reports/) for the full flow.

<PromptBox label="Outline a report">
I'm going to write a report on [topic] for [audience: e.g., executives, teammates].
Purpose: [request a decision / share status / make a proposal]
Length: [number] pages
First, suggest a table of contents and a key sentence for each section. Once I confirm, we'll draft section by section. Don't include any numbers that aren't in the materials I give you.
Reference materials: [paste materials]
</PromptBox>

### 3. Summarize Excel and CSV data

**What it can do**: Upload a table and it finds totals, trends, and odd values, and can also suggest Excel formulas.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI that can analyze files. See [Let AI analyze your Excel and CSV data](/chat/03-data-analysis/).

<PromptBox label="Explore the data">
The attached table is data about [what the data is].
1) First check the column layout, blank cells, duplicates, and odd values.
2) Summarize [monthly / by-department] totals and changes in a table.
3) Tell me 3 notable changes and questions worth checking.
Write down how you calculated things so I can verify them.
</PromptBox>

### 4. Write emails and announcements

**What it can do**: Draft emails with a tricky tone, such as requests, apologies, reminders, and announcements.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. To use it inside Gmail, see [Practical use of AI features (Gemini) in Google Workspace](/automation/19-google-workspace-gemini/).

<PromptBox label="Email draft">
Write an email for the situation below.
Recipient: [title, relationship]
Purpose: [request / scheduling / delay notice / reminder]
Must include: [dates, what's being requested]
Tone: polite but concise, within 5 sentences. Also give me 3 subject line options.
</PromptBox>

### 5. Morning briefing and schedule planning

**What it can do**: Read your email and calendar and organize today's to-dos and the emails you need to reply to.
**Suggested tools**: Claude connectors and similar. Follow [Connect Gmail and Calendar to AI for a morning briefing](/automation/17-morning-briefing/) for setup.

<PromptBox label="Organize today's to-dos">
Look at today's schedule and the emails that have come in since yesterday, and organize the following.
1) The 3 things I must do today
2) Emails that need a reply, and their priority
3) What to prepare before meetings
Only read my email. Don't send or delete anything.
</PromptBox>

## Copy-ready prompts for office and admin roles

### Meetings to action items

<PromptBox label="Meeting notes to action items">
Turn the meeting notes below into: 1) a 3-line summary, 2) decisions made, 3) an action item table with owner, task, and due date, 4) open questions. If an owner or date is not mentioned, write 'not stated' instead of guessing.

[Paste the notes]
</PromptBox>

Tip: See [AI meeting notes](/automation/37-meeting-ai/) for how to get clean notes first.

<PromptBox label="Meeting agenda from a goal">
Draft a [30]-minute meeting agenda. Meeting goal: [what must be decided]. Attendees: [roles]. Background: [2 or 3 lines]. Give time boxes per item, who leads each, and what people should read beforehand. Add a closing step to confirm action items.
</PromptBox>

Tip: Specific goals give tighter agendas, as in [Asking good questions](/basics/03-asking-well/).

<PromptBox label="Follow-up email after a meeting">
Write a short follow-up email to the attendees of [meeting name]. Include: thanks in one line, the decisions, the action items with owners and dates (from the list below), and the next meeting date [date]. Tone: [friendly and professional]. Under 150 words.

[Paste the action items]
</PromptBox>

Tip: More email patterns are in [Write emails and messages with AI](/chat/42-emails-and-messages/).

### Reports and plans

<PromptBox label="Report outline from raw notes">
Organize my notes below into a report outline for [audience, e.g., my director]. Purpose: [decide / inform / request budget]. Give 5 to 7 section headings, 2 bullets under each, and mark any section where my notes are missing information.

[Paste your notes]
</PromptBox>

Tip: The drafting flow is in [Write and polish report and proposal drafts](/chat/04-write-reports/).

<PromptBox label="Shorten a long document">
Summarize the document below for a busy reader in 3 levels: one sentence, one paragraph, and a bullet list of key points with page or section references. Then list anything that looks unclear or contradictory. Do not add information that is not in the document.

[Paste the text, with confidential details removed]
</PromptBox>

Tip: See [Summarize long documents](/chat/02-summarize-documents/) for handling very long files.

<PromptBox label="Plain-language rewrite for non-experts">
Rewrite the text below so that someone outside our department can understand it. Replace jargon with plain words, keep it to [number] sentences, and keep all numbers and dates exactly as they are. Mark any term you were unsure about.

[Paste the text]
</PromptBox>

Tip: Use the review habit from [Can you trust AI answers?](/basics/04-fact-checking/) before sending.

### Spreadsheets and routine tasks

<PromptBox label="Explain and write an Excel formula">
I use [Excel / Google Sheets]. Here is my sheet layout: column A is [description], column B is [description], and the data starts in row 2. I want to [goal, e.g., sum amounts for one department in one month]. Write the formula, explain each part in plain words, and give one example row to test it.
</PromptBox>

Tip: More spreadsheet techniques are in [Spreadsheet AI](/automation/23-spreadsheet-ai/).

<PromptBox label="Clean up messy data">
Here is a sample of my data: [paste 10 rows with no personal details]. Problems I see: [duplicates, mixed date formats, extra spaces]. Suggest step-by-step cleanup using [Excel / Google Sheets] features, and tell me how to double-check the result before I overwrite the original.
</PromptBox>

Tip: Keep the original file safe, and see [Analyze Excel and CSV data](/chat/03-data-analysis/).

<PromptBox label="Plan my week from a messy list">
Here is my to-do list and my calendar for this week: [paste]. Sort the tasks by urgency and effort, suggest which to do in which time slot, and flag anything that conflicts with a meeting. Keep it as a table, and tell me which 3 tasks matter most.
</PromptBox>

Tip: For daily routines, try [Morning briefing](/automation/17-morning-briefing/).

## What to watch out for

<Aside type="caution" title="Before you paste in internal materials">
- **Company and personal information**: Don't casually paste in performance reviews, salaries, customer lists, or contracts. Check your company's AI usage policy first, and mask names and contact details. The standards are in [Using AI while protecting personal and company information](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).
- **Numbers and facts**: Check any numbers AI calculates or summarizes against the original, and do the same for laws, regulations, and dates. The method is in [Can you trust AI answers?](/basics/04-fact-checking/).
- **Email and calendar connections**: Grant read access first, and decide carefully about send and delete permissions. See [How to use personal AI agents safely](/agents/05-agent-safety/).
- **Copyright**: Don't copy external reports or articles word for word, and keep track of your sources.
</Aside>
