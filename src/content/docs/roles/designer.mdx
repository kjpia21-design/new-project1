## Who this is for

This page suits people who make visuals for a living or as part of their job, such as graphic designers, in-house designers, content designers, and people who handle both design and promotion.

- Thumbnails, banners, and promo images are needed again and again, and each one starts from a blank page.
- You'd like quick rough visuals to show a direction before spending hours on a polished version.
- You're unsure how far you can go with AI-made images when it comes to copyright and rules.

## Top 5 ways AI helps

### 1. Explore concepts and mood directions

**What it can do**: Turn a vague brief into several directions you can compare, with keywords, color ideas, and layout notes.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. Learn how to structure instructions in [Writing Work Prompts: Setting Role, Context, and Format](/chat/01-prompt-basics/).

<PromptBox label="Explore directions">
I'm designing [what: e.g., an event poster] for [audience].
Purpose: [what it should achieve]
Give me 3 different visual directions. For each, include a mood in one line, color ideas, layout notes, and what to avoid.
Ask me anything you need to know first.
</PromptBox>

### 2. Generate business images

**What it can do**: Create thumbnails and promo images from a written description, then refine them step by step.
**Suggested tools**: An image generation AI. Follow [Business Image Generation: Make Thumbnails and Promo Images](/media/11-business-images/), and go further with [Advanced Image Generation: Get the Images You Want from Gemini and ChatGPT](/media/30-image-generation-advanced/).

<PromptBox label="Image prompt">
Create an image for [use: e.g., a blog thumbnail].
Subject: [what to show]
Style: [flat illustration / photo-like / etc.]
Colors: [palette]
Composition: leave empty space on the [left/right] for text.
Don't include any text or logos in the image.
</PromptBox>

### 3. Make a set of social media content

**What it can do**: Produce a matching set of posts and cards in one look.
**Suggested tools**: Canva AI. See [Make a Set of Social Media Content with Canva AI](/media/12-canva-sns/).

<PromptBox label="Plan a post set">
Plan a set of 5 social media posts about [topic] for [audience].
For each post, give a headline of 15 characters or fewer, a one-line supporting message, and a visual idea.
Keep the tone consistent across the set.
</PromptBox>

### 4. Draft presentation slides

**What it can do**: Build a slide outline and a first draft that you then refine in your own design.
**Suggested tools**: Presentation AI tools. Compare them in [Make Presentations with AI: Gamma vs. Claude vs. Copilot](/media/25-ai-presentations/).

<PromptBox label="Slide outline">
I need a [number]-slide presentation about [topic] for [audience].
Suggest the flow first: one line per slide with its key message and a suggested visual.
Once I confirm the flow, we'll write each slide.
</PromptBox>

### 5. Try short video ideas

**What it can do**: Sketch a storyboard and produce short promo clips.
**Suggested tools**: AI video tools. See [Make a Short Promo Video with AI Video Tools](/media/13-promo-video/) and [Make Short AI Videos with Google's Video Tools](/media/34-ai-video-generation/).

<PromptBox label="Storyboard">
Write a storyboard for a [length]-second promo video about [product or event].
For each scene, give the visual, the on-screen text, and the duration.
Keep it simple enough to produce with an AI video tool.
</PromptBox>

## What to watch out for

<Aside type="caution" title="Before you publish AI-made visuals">
- **Copyright and rights**: Check how generated images may be used, and don't imitate a specific person's or brand's work. The basics are in [Can You Use AI Output? Copyright and Ethics Basics](/basics/09-ai-copyright-ethics/).
- **Client and unreleased materials**: Don't casually upload unreleased designs, client assets, or photos of people. Check your contract and company policy first. The standards are in [Using AI While Protecting Personal and Company Information](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).
- **Text in images**: Check any text, logos, and details in generated images yourself, and fix them by hand.
- **Facts in copy**: Check any claims, numbers, and names in the text you put on visuals. The method is in [Can You Trust AI Answers? How to Fact-Check](/basics/04-fact-checking/).
</Aside>
