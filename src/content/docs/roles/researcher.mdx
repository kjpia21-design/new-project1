## Who this is for

This page suits researchers, graduate students, and anyone whose work is gathering information, reading it, and turning it into writing.

- Reading piles of papers and documents takes more time than you can spare.
- Searching for information and checking its sources is a job in itself.
- Cleaning up data and drafting reports and translations keeps repeating.

## Top 5 ways AI helps

### 1. Read and summarize long documents

**What it can do**: Summarize a long document and pull out key points, and then check them against the source.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. See [Summarizing meeting notes and long documents, and pulling out action items](/chat/02-summarize-documents/). To ask questions about only your own materials, see [Studying and Organizing with Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)](/chat/21-notebooklm/).

<PromptBox label="Summarize a document">
Below is a document I'm reading. Organize it in this format.
1) The document's purpose in one line
2) Key points (numbered list)
3) Terms I should look up
4) Parts that are unclear or that I should check against the original
Don't add anything that isn't in the document.

[Paste document]
</PromptBox>

### 2. Search for information and check sources

**What it can do**: Gather information with AI search and open the cited sources to check them yourself.
**Suggested tools**: See [Using AI Search Well: Perplexity vs. ChatGPT Search vs. Claude Web Search](/chat/22-ai-search/) and [Research: web search and deep investigation](/claude/c10-research/).

<PromptBox label="Search with sources">
I want to find out about [topic].
1) Give me an overview in 5 lines.
2) Attach a source link to each claim.
3) Separate what is well established from what is uncertain.
I will open the links and check them myself.
</PromptBox>

### 3. Explore and analyze data

**What it can do**: Upload a table to check blanks, duplicates, and odd values, then summarize it.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI that can analyze files. See [Handing Excel and CSV Data Analysis to AI](/chat/03-data-analysis/). For spreadsheets, there is also [Adding AI to Excel and Google Sheets](/automation/23-spreadsheet-ai/).

<PromptBox label="Explore the data">
The attached table is data about [what the data is].
1) First check the column layout, blank cells, duplicates, and odd values.
2) Summarize the totals and changes in a table.
3) Tell me 3 notable points worth checking.
Write down how you calculated things so I can verify them.
</PromptBox>

### 4. Draft reports and papers

**What it can do**: Set up an outline, write a draft for each section, and adjust the tone for the reader.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. Follow [Drafting and Polishing Reports and Proposals](/chat/04-write-reports/) for the full flow.

<PromptBox label="Outline a report">
I'm going to write a report on [topic] for [audience].
Purpose: [share results / make a proposal]
First, suggest a table of contents and a key sentence for each section. Once I confirm, we'll draft section by section. Don't include any numbers that aren't in the materials I give you.
Reference materials: [paste materials]
</PromptBox>

### 5. Translate documents

**What it can do**: Translate documents and review the result.
**Suggested tools**: See [AI Translation in Practice: Translate Work Documents with DeepL, Papago, and Claude](/media/26-ai-translation/).

<PromptBox label="Translate and review">
Translate the text below into [language]. Keep names and technical terms as they are, and list any sentences you were unsure about at the end so I can check them.

[Paste text]
</PromptBox>

## What to watch out for

<Aside type="caution" title="Before you rely on AI output">
- **Facts and sources**: AI can be plausibly wrong. Open the cited sources and check them yourself. The method is in [Can you trust AI answers?](/basics/04-fact-checking/).
- **Unpublished and confidential materials**: Don't casually paste in unpublished data, personal information, or confidential documents. See [Using AI while protecting personal and company information](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).
- **Numbers**: Check any numbers AI calculates or summarizes against the original data.
- **Copyright and ethics**: Keep track of your sources and check the rules before publishing. See [Can you use AI output? Copyright and ethics basics](/basics/09-ai-copyright-ethics/).
</Aside>
