## How to use this section

1. On your role's page, skim the **Top 5 use cases** and copy a prompt you like to try on the work you're doing right now.
2. Once you're comfortable, follow the tutorials in the **recommended learning path** in order.
3. Before you put work materials into AI, be sure to read **What to watch out for**.

If you're new to AI, we recommend starting with [Your first 30 minutes with AI](/basics/01-first-30-minutes/). If you meet a word you don't know, keep the [AI glossary](/start/glossary/) open.
