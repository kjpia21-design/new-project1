## Who this is for

This page suits people who work on people operations, such as HR generalists, recruiters, hiring managers, and training or onboarding staff.

- Interviews, meetings, and one-on-ones leave behind notes that need organizing.
- Job posts, announcements, policies, and emails take time to write and revise.
- Much of your work involves sensitive personal information, so you need to be careful about what goes into AI.

## Top 5 ways AI helps

### 1. Organize meeting and interview notes

**What it can do**: Turn your notes or a transcript into a clear summary with decisions, owners, and next steps.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. For recordings, first turn them into text with [Turn Meeting and Lecture Recordings into Organized Text](/media/14-transcribe-recordings/). Summarizing tips are in [Summarizing Meeting Notes and Long Documents, and Pulling Out Action Items](/chat/02-summarize-documents/).

<PromptBox label="Organize notes">
Below are my notes from a meeting. Organize them in this format.
1) Purpose in one line
2) Decisions (numbered list)
3) Action items table: task, owner, deadline
4) Open issues
If something isn't in the notes, write "TBD" and don't make it up.
I have removed names and other personal details.

[Paste notes]
</PromptBox>

### 2. Draft job posts, announcements, and policy documents

**What it can do**: Set up an outline and write a first draft that you then edit for accuracy and tone.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. Follow [Drafting and Polishing Reports and Proposals](/chat/04-write-reports/) for the full flow.

<PromptBox label="Draft a document">
I'm writing a [job post / internal announcement / guideline] for [audience].
Purpose: [what it should achieve]
Include: [facts I will provide]
First suggest an outline. Once I confirm, write the draft. Don't add any conditions, numbers, or rules that I haven't given you.
</PromptBox>

### 3. Write emails and messages to staff and candidates

**What it can do**: Draft emails with a delicate tone, such as notices, reminders, and replies.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. To use it inside Gmail, see [Using Google Workspace's AI Features (Gemini) at Work](/automation/19-google-workspace-gemini/).

<PromptBox label="Email draft">
Write an email for the situation below.
Recipient: [role, relationship]
Purpose: [scheduling / notice / reminder / reply]
Must include: [dates, what's being requested]
Tone: polite and clear, within 5 sentences. Also give me 3 subject line options.
Don't include any personal details beyond what I wrote here.
</PromptBox>

### 4. Translate documents for international teams

**What it can do**: Translate work documents and check the results.
**Suggested tools**: Translation tools and chat AI. See [AI Translation in Practice: Translate Work Documents with DeepL, Papago, and Claude](/media/26-ai-translation/).

<PromptBox label="Translate and check">
Translate the text below into [language] for [audience].
Keep the tone [formal / friendly]. Mark any phrases whose meaning is unclear instead of guessing.
Then list the 3 places I should have a native speaker double-check.

[Paste text with personal details removed]
</PromptBox>

### 5. Keep documents and schedules organized

**What it can do**: Tidy up accumulated documents and plan your day from your email and calendar.
**Suggested tools**: Notion AI and connectors. See [Organizing Work Documents and Databases with Notion AI](/automation/16-notion-ai/) and [Connecting Gmail and Calendar to AI for a Morning Briefing](/automation/17-morning-briefing/).

<PromptBox label="Plan today">
Look at today's schedule and the emails that have come in since yesterday, and organize the following.
1) The 3 things I must do today
2) Emails that need a reply, and their priority
3) What to prepare before each meeting
Only read my email. Don't send or delete anything.
</PromptBox>

## What to watch out for

<Aside type="caution" title="Before you paste in people-related materials">
- **Personal information**: Don't paste in names, contact details, performance reviews, salaries, health information, or candidate files. Remove or mask details, and check your company's AI usage policy first. The standards are in [Using AI While Protecting Personal and Company Information](/basics/05-privacy-safety/), and settings are in [Turn Off AI Training on Your Chats: Privacy Settings in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini](/basics/12-privacy-settings/).
- **Fairness in hiring and evaluation**: Don't let AI make or decide hiring or evaluation judgments for you. Use it for drafting and organizing, and make the decisions yourself, following your company's rules and the law. Be aware that AI can reflect bias, as noted in [Can You Use AI Output? Copyright and Ethics Basics](/basics/09-ai-copyright-ethics/).
- **Facts, rules, and laws**: Check any legal or policy claims against official sources. The method is in [Can You Trust AI Answers? How to Fact-Check](/basics/04-fact-checking/).
- **Email and calendar connections**: Grant read access first, and decide carefully about send and delete permissions. See [How to use personal AI agents safely](/agents/05-agent-safety/).
</Aside>
