## Who this is for

This page suits people who spend the day talking and writing to customers, such as sales reps, account managers, and customer support staff.

- Replying to emails and inquiries takes up much of the day.
- Notes from customer meetings and calls need to be written up afterward.
- Proposals and follow-ups are always similar, yet each one takes time.

## Top 5 ways AI helps

### 1. Summarize email threads and draft replies

**What it can do**: Summarize a long thread and draft a reply in the tone you need.
**Suggested tools**: See [Organizing Your Inbox with AI: Gemini in Gmail, Copilot in Outlook, and Claude Connectors](/automation/38-email-ai/).

<PromptBox label="Reply draft">
Below is an email thread with a customer. Do the following.
1) Summarize the thread in 3 lines.
2) List what the customer is asking for.
3) Write a reply draft: polite but concise, within 5 sentences.
Don't promise dates or prices that aren't in the thread. Mark them as [to be confirmed].

[Paste thread]
</PromptBox>

### 2. Organize meeting notes and pull out to-dos

**What it can do**: Extract decisions, owners, and deadlines into a table.
**Suggested tools**: See [Summarizing meeting notes and long documents, and pulling out action items](/chat/02-summarize-documents/) and [Comparing Online Meeting AI](/automation/37-meeting-ai/).

<PromptBox label="Organize meeting notes">
Below are my notes from a customer meeting. Organize them in this format.
1) The meeting's purpose in one line
2) What the customer asked for
3) Action items table: task, owner, deadline
If an owner or deadline isn't in the notes, write "TBD" and don't make one up.

[Paste notes]
</PromptBox>

### 3. Draft proposals and presentations

**What it can do**: Set up an outline and write a draft, then turn it into slides.
**Suggested tools**: See [Drafting and Polishing Reports and Proposals](/chat/04-write-reports/) and [Make Presentations with AI](/media/25-ai-presentations/).

<PromptBox label="Outline a proposal">
I'm going to write a proposal on [offering] for [customer type].
Purpose: [what I want the customer to decide]
First, suggest a table of contents and a key sentence for each section. Once I confirm, we'll draft section by section. Don't include any numbers that aren't in the materials I give you.
Reference materials: [paste materials]
</PromptBox>

### 4. Catch up on team messages

**What it can do**: Summarize conversations and search for answers inside your work messenger.
**Suggested tools**: See [Adding AI to Your Work Messenger: Slack AI and Claude Tag](/automation/41-slack-ai/).

<PromptBox label="Catch up on a thread">
Below is a long team conversation. Summarize it in 5 lines, then list the decisions and the open questions separately.

[Paste conversation]
</PromptBox>

### 5. Automate routine follow-ups

**What it can do**: Connect email, sheets, and notifications so repeated steps run on their own.
**Suggested tools**: See [Automating Email → Sheet → Notification with Zapier/Make](/automation/18-zapier-make/) and [Connecting Gmail and Calendar to AI for a Morning Briefing](/automation/17-morning-briefing/).

<PromptBox label="Morning to-dos">
Look at today's schedule and the emails that have come in since yesterday, and organize the following.
1) The 3 things I must do today
2) Emails that need a reply, and their priority
Only read my email. Don't send or delete anything.
</PromptBox>

## What to watch out for

<Aside type="caution" title="Before you paste in customer information">
- **Customer and company information**: Don't casually paste in customer lists, contracts, or personal details. Check your company's AI usage policy first, and mask names and contact details. See [Using AI while protecting personal and company information](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).
- **Promises and numbers**: Check prices, dates, and terms in any draft against the original before sending. See [Can you trust AI answers?](/basics/04-fact-checking/).
- **Send and delete permissions**: Grant read access first, and decide carefully about send and delete permissions. See [How to use personal AI agents safely](/agents/05-agent-safety/).
- **Recordings**: Check consent and company rules before using meeting recording and summary features. See [Comparing Online Meeting AI](/automation/37-meeting-ai/).
</Aside>
