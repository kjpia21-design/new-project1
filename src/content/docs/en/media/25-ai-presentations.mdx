<Summary>
- All three tools work the same basic way: **you describe a topic and the AI makes slides (asking questions first if needed)**. Gamma shows you a plan and asks for approval. The result is a draft, so a person needs to edit it.
- **Gamma** is an AI service dedicated to presentations. **Claude** makes slides in chat with the Slides template and exports to PowerPoint. **Copilot** builds them right inside PowerPoint.
- If you have to follow a company template, Copilot is convenient because you **start from the template file**; for a quick draft, Gamma is convenient.
- AI features use **credits and usage**. Gamma's free plan credits don't renew (as of 2026-10-19).
- AI can get numbers, sources, and proper nouns wrong. Always check them yourself before presenting.
</Summary>

## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Organize your topic and audience and have the AI make an **outline first**.
- Pick the right tool for your situation among Gamma, Claude, and Copilot in PowerPoint.
- Export your draft as a **PowerPoint file (.pptx)** and finish it.
- Check facts, numbers, and image rights before presenting.

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Outline** | A skeleton that lists only slide titles and key points. Editing it before you make the slides is the most efficient approach. |
| **Gamma** | A separate service that makes presentations, documents, and web pages with AI. What it creates is called a "gamma." |
| **Claude Slides** | One of Claude's Artifacts templates. It makes presentations from notes, reports, or the conversation. |
| **Copilot (PowerPoint)** | The AI assistant inside PowerPoint. It may not appear depending on your Microsoft 365 subscription and your organization's settings. |
| **Credits** | The unit of usage consumed when you use AI features. Each service calculates it differently. |

### How do the three tools differ?

| Comparison | Gamma | Claude Slides | Copilot in PowerPoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Where you start | gamma.app dashboard | Chat or the Artifacts tab | Copilot Chat inside PowerPoint |
| Workflow | Questions -> review plan -> build (Plan mode) | Ask in chat, then request edits | Enter a description -> (questions about audience, length, etc., which you can skip) -> slides generated |
| Export | PDF, PNG, PPTX, etc. | PowerPoint, PDF, Google Slides | Already a PowerPoint file |
| Company template | Separate themes | Upload an existing deck and ask it to match the style | Start from the template file to keep the theme |
| Cost structure | Credits (free plan doesn't renew) | Used within your Claude plan (usage amounts aren't published) | A Microsoft 365 subscription that includes Copilot (we couldn't confirm pricing) |

## Step by step

The example is "a 15-minute presentation on information security training for new hires." Steps 1-2 are the same whichever tool you use.

<Steps>

1. **Write down the skeleton of your presentation first**

   Before handing it off to AI, write down just four things: the topic, the audience, the presentation length, and the content that must be included. For a 15-minute talk, around 10 slides is usually about right (it varies by person, so there's no single right answer).

   <PromptBox>
   I'm making a 15-minute information security training presentation for 20 new hires. Keep it to 10 slides or fewer. It must cover (1) how to spot phishing emails, (2) password management, and (3) the reporting procedure when an incident happens. First, show me just an outline with a title and key points for each slide. Once I've checked it, make the slides.
   </PromptBox>

2. **Edit the outline**

   At the outline stage, reorder slides and delete any you don't want. Editing after the slides are all made costs more credits and throws the design off.

3. **Make a draft with a tool (pick one of the three)**

   **A. Gamma**: Write your topic in the prompt bar on the dashboard and choose a mode. **Plan** builds after asking questions and letting you review a plan (the default), and **Express** makes a draft right away. **Research** gathers web sources first and is available only in Plan. You can also paste notes or attach a file. If the questions are a hassle, you can choose "Decide for me."

   **B. Claude**: Ask for a presentation in chat, or choose the Slides template under Output in the message box. If you upload notes or a report with it, it builds from that content. After it's made, you can edit the slides directly or ask Claude for changes.

   **C. Copilot in PowerPoint**: Follow the "hardest part" procedure below.

   <FlowSteps title="Make a new presentation with Copilot in PowerPoint" officialUrl="https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/PowerPoint/copilot/create-a-new-presentation-with-copilot-in-powerpoint" steps={[

     { where: 'Copilot', action: 'Wait while it gathers material and builds with the template you chose', result: 'Slides are generated' },
   ]} />

   If you don't see Copilot, it may not be included in your subscription or organization settings. In that case, ask your administrator. As of 2026-10-26, the official help no longer has the Add Content > Agent Mode step it described earlier, and instead describes the model selector (default Auto) and the Brand Kit selection step. If your screen differs, follow the latest help and what you actually see.

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Requesting a presentation from the model selector and input box in Copilot Chat" officialUrl="https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/PowerPoint/copilot/create-a-new-presentation-with-copilot-in-powerpoint" />

4. **Check the slides yourself**

   - Compare numbers, dates, names of laws and regulations, and people's names against the source.
   - If a slide has too much text, ask to trim it or split it up.
   - AI-made images may not match the real situation. Replace any that show people's faces or other people's logos.
   - As of 2026-10-26, this help page doesn't confirm source labeling for AI images made by Copilot (an earlier check said "AI-generated" was shown in the speaker notes). Check the speaker notes and images yourself.

5. **Export and finish**

   - **Gamma**: Choose PDF, PNG, or PowerPoint under Share (top right) > Export. You need to choose "All cards" to get everything. On the free plan, exporting to PDF or PPTX adds a "Made by Gamma" badge. It disappears on paid plans (Plus, Pro, Ultra).
   - **Claude**: Choose PowerPoint, PDF, or Google Slides under Export.
   - **Copilot**: It's already a PowerPoint file, so just save.

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Choosing PowerPoint from Gamma's Share > Export menu" officialUrl="https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/8022861-what-s-the-easiest-way-to-export-my-gamma" />

</Steps>

## Quick reference

### Gamma plan summary (as of 2026-10-19, only items confirmed in the official help)

| Item | Free | Plus | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI credits | Don't renew (400 on sign-up, 200 per referral, up to 2,000 in the account through referrals) | 1,000 per month | 4,000 per month |
| Slides generated at once | Up to 10 | Up to 100 | Up to 100 |
| Export badge | "Made by Gamma" shown | Removed | Removed |
| Price | Not verified in this guide | Not verified in this guide | Not verified in this guide |

Credits are used per slide and per image, and longer or more advanced models use more. Unused plan credits on paid plans roll over up to 2x the plan size. See your balance in the credit display at the top right of the screen.

### What can change when you export from Gamma

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Formats | PDF, PNG, PowerPoint (PPTX), Google Slides (PPTX upload), post to LinkedIn |
| Tables | Come out as editable tables in PPTX (can be turned off) |
| Speaker notes | Included in PPTX and Google Slides exports |
| Fonts | Embedded in the PPTX. In rare cases where that fails, they switch to fonts installed on your PC |
| Slide master | Not included. Formatting is applied slide by slide |
| Gradient titles and some effects | May look different or use substitute styles |

### Tool selection guide

| Situation | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| I need a good-looking draft fast | Gamma |
| I already organized a report with Claude | Claude Slides (see [C6. Artifacts](/en/claude/c06-artifacts/)) |
| I must use a company template | Copilot in PowerPoint (start from the template file) |
| I need to turn a Word report into a presentation | Copilot (it understands structure well if you've used Word styles; documents under 24 MB recommended) |

## Common sticking points

### I don't see the Copilot icon or Brand Kit selection

- **Cause**: Copilot may not be included in your Microsoft 365 subscription or organization settings, or the feature may not have rolled out to your account yet.
- **Fix**: Follow the official help's "check which Copilot license you have" guidance, or ask your administrator. As an alternative, you can make a draft with Gamma or Claude first.

### I'm running short on credits on Gamma's free plan

- **Cause**: The free plan's credits aren't refilled monthly.
- **Fix**: Finalize a short outline and generate in one go to cut waste. You can choose to make it without images (Don't add images) or use image placeholders. The credit cost of image generation is shown in advance when you pick a model. If you'll keep using it, compare the paid plans.

### The text style or effects in the exported PPTX look different

- **Cause**: PowerPoint doesn't support some of Gamma's effects (gradients, translucent backgrounds) as is.
- **Fix**: Open the important slides in PowerPoint, check them by eye, and fix them. If it needs to look exactly the same, export to PDF. When sharing on screen as is, you can also present from a Gamma link.

### There's too much text on the slides

- **Cause**: Without a request to condense, AI tends to write long sentences.
- **Fix**: Ask for "3 key lines per slide, about 25 characters per line, and detailed explanations in the speaker notes."

## Practical tips

- **Spend your time at the outline stage.** It's easier than fixing slides after they're made.
- **Paste in your materials.** Giving the AI source material reduces made-up content. It can still be wrong, so check it. ([How to fact-check](/en/basics/04-fact-checking/))
- **Mask personal information and confidential details before the material goes outside.** In Gamma's personal workspaces (Free, Plus, Pro, Ultra), the default allows content to be used for AI training, and you can turn it off in the Data Controls tab in account settings. Organization workspaces such as Business and Enterprise are described as never used for training (as of 2026-10-14). For the full principles, see [Protecting Personal and Company Information](/en/basics/05-privacy-safety/).
- **Check image rights.** Don't use AI images that show real people or other people's logos. For commercial-use terms of image generation tools, see also [Tutorial 12](/en/media/12-canva-sns/).
- **Practice the presentation yourself.** You can have AI draft the speaker notes, but read them aloud and put them in your own voice.
