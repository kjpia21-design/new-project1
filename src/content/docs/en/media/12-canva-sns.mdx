## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Create a Brand Kit (a Pro-or-higher plan feature) to lock in your logo, colors, and fonts.
- Make 3 Instagram posts and 1 story with Magic Design and Magic Media.
- Check and conserve your AI usage.
- Review the items to confirm before commercial use (ownership, permission for people and brands).

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Canva AI** | The bundle of AI features inside Canva. It supports several languages including Korean, though some features may support fewer languages. |
| **Magic Design** | A feature that generates design templates from a description you enter. |
| **Magic Media** | A feature that creates images, graphics, and short videos from a description. It's classified as a premium AI tool and uses your monthly AI usage. |
| **Brand Kit** | A place to keep your logo, color palette, and fonts together. |
| **AI usage allowance** | The monthly AI usage limit set by plan. It resets every month, and Standard, Premium, and Ultra tools share a single allowance. |

### How is this different from image generation tools?

The Gemini app in [Tutorial 11](/en/media/11-business-images/) is strong at making **a single image**, while Canva is convenient for handling **text, layout, and multiple formats** all at once. A common combination is to create images in Gemini and upload them to Canva.

1. **Check your AI usage**

   Under profile icon > Settings > Billing, you can reportedly see your **AI usage** and how much is left. We couldn't confirm the free plan's allowance from the official pages, so check it on screen.

2. **Create a Brand Kit**

   In the **Brand** section of the home screen side panel, choose **Create new Brand Kit** and add your logo, colors, and fonts. See "Brand Kit in detail" below first for plan requirements.

3. **Make your first design with Magic Design**

   Describe the design you want to Canva AI. If your Brand Kit has brand templates, Canva AI reportedly can use them as a reference to make an on-brand design.

   <PromptBox>
   Create an Instagram feed post announcing the launch of our new café menu item, the "Yuja Cream Latte." Square format. Warm beige and brown tones. The title is "Yuja Cream Latte Now Available" and the subtitle is "All October: $1 off your first cup." Leave a spot for a photo.
   </PromptBox>

4. **Create an image with Magic Media**

   Open the image tab in **Magic Media** in the side panel, enter a description, and generate. The options are listed under "Magic Media usage notes" below.

   <PromptBox>
   A cup of yuja cream latte on a wooden table, with a slice of yuja citrus next to it. Natural light, warm tones, shot from directly above. No people and no logos.
   </PromptBox>

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Entering a description in the Magic Media image tab and choosing a result" />

5. **Expand it into a set**

   Make 3 feed posts (new menu intro, price and offer, store info) and 1 story using the same brand colors and fonts. If you copy an existing design and change only the content, the set stays consistent. For where to find the resize feature that converts to other formats (such as vertical story), follow the on-screen guidance.

6. **Check before you export**

   - Are there any identifiable people, other people's logos, or characters in the images?
   - Did a person verify factual details like names, prices, and dates? (AI-written text can be wrong)
   - Does the post include the disclosures an ad needs (price, dates, conditions)?

   After checking, export via Share or Download.

</Steps>

### Brand Kit in detail

In the home screen side panel, select **Brand**, choose **Create new Brand Kit** from the Brand Kit dropdown, name it, upload your logo, and add a color palette and fonts. Canva's developer docs (checked 2026-10-13) say Brand Kit is available to Canva Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, and the developer's Google Play listing also includes Brand, which stores logos, fonts, and colors, among the Canva Pro features. Free accounts aren't listed as eligible, so if you're on the free plan, first check whether the Brand menu appears on your screen. Only owners, admins, and brand designers can edit a team's brand colors.

### Magic Media usage notes

Open **Magic Media** in the editor side panel (if you don't see it, search for it in Apps). In the image tab, enter a description, choose a style (photo, digital art, fine art) and layout (square, landscape, portrait), and click **Generate images**. For a result you like, you can use **Generate more like this** in the More menu to create similar images, and clicking one adds it to your design.


## Quick reference

### Magic Media options

| Item | Details (per Canva Help) |
| --- | --- |
| Image styles | Photo, digital art, fine art (you can also upload a reference image) |
| Graphic styles | Simple, artistic, detailed |
| Layout | Square, landscape, portrait |
| Making variations | More > Generate more like this |
| Video | Magic Media video is reportedly a short, silent clip. Magic Video is a separate feature |

### Ownership and commercial use (summary of Canva AI terms)

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Ownership of output | You own it. However, if you modified or included Canva library content, a license applies |
| Similar results | Other users may receive similar results |
| Scope of use | Allowed for lawful purposes, but it's **your responsibility**. Getting professional advice before commercial use is recommended |
| People, characters, brands | Identifiable ones require permission from the rights holder, which Canva can't give |
| Copyright protection | In many countries (including the US), copyright protection for AI-generated works may be limited |
| Products for sale | AI-generated images aren't covered by the Canva Content License Agreement and follow the AI terms. Photos edited with AI can reportedly be sold only as part of an original design |
| Indemnity (IP indemnity) | AI indemnity is described for enterprise customers. We couldn't confirm it for personal and Pro plans, so see the terms |

<Aside type="caution" title="Likeness rights and brands">
Don't use faces that look like real people, famous characters, or other companies' trademarks. If you want to edit photos of employees or customers with AI and use them in promotion, get the person's consent first.
</Aside>

## Common sticking points

### I can't find Magic Media

- **Cause**: The menu location may have changed, or your plan may not support it.
- **Fix**: Search for Magic Media in Apps in the editor. If it isn't there, check the on-screen guidance and the help pages.

### AI features are locked (usage limit reached)

- **Cause**: When you use up your monthly AI allowance, the tiers you can use get blocked. It resets every month.
- **Fix**: Check what's left under Settings > Billing, then wait for the reset or compare plans.

### I don't like the Korean fonts

- **Cause**: The default font may not suit Korean designs.
- **Fix**: Set the fonts to use in your Brand Kit. The range of Korean fonts available differs by plan, so check on screen.

### The text in the image is garbled

- **Cause**: Text in AI images may not be accurate.
- **Fix**: Ask for no text in the image, and add the text using Canva's text tool.

## Practical tips

- **Create your Brand Kit first.** It cuts down the time you spend picking colors each time.
- **Pick one image style and stick with it.** The set will feel much more unified.
- **To conserve your AI allowance**, write specific descriptions and create variations from results you like.
- **Have a person verify factual details.** Typos in prices and dates can turn into advertising disputes.
- **If you're on a team**, Brand Controls reportedly let you restrict people to approved colors and fonts.
