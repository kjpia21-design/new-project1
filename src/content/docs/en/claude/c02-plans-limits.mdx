<Summary>
- Paid plans (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, Enterprise) have a **session limit that resets every 5 hours** and a **weekly limit that resets once a week**. Verified Ultra accounts have no 5-hour limit.
- Individual plans are Free, Pro ($20/month, or $17/month billed annually), Max ($100/month, 5x Pro), and **Ultra ($200/month, 10x Pro weekly usage)**. The old "Max 20x" no longer exists. (as of 2026-10-31)
- The Claude apps (web, desktop, mobile) and Claude Code **share the same limits.**
- **Absolute numbers**, like how many messages or tokens you get, are not published officially. Check your own usage in Settings > Usage.
- When you use up your limit, instead of stopping you can keep going on a pay-as-you-go basis with **extra usage (usage credits)**. You have to turn it on yourself.
- Prices and limits change often, so read this document as **of 2026-10-31**. (Prices are US prices.)
</Summary>

## What you'll be able to do

- Pick the plan that fits you.
- Explain how the session limit, weekly limit, and extra usage connect.
- Check your own usage and the next reset time yourself.
- Decide whether to wait, switch models, or turn on extra usage when you hit a limit.

## Concepts to know first

<PlanFlowDiagram />

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Session limit** | A limit that resets every 5 hours. It applies to paid plans, but verified Ultra accounts have no 5-hour limit. |
| **Weekly limit** | A limit that resets once a week. It applies to all models, and the reset time is a fixed time set per account. Check the next reset in Settings > Usage. |
| **Extra usage (usage credits)** | A feature that, instead of blocking you after you use up your limit, lets you keep going **pay as you go** at standard API rates. You top up in advance (prepaid). The official docs also call it "extra usage." |
| **Seat** | The spot you give one member in Team or Enterprise. Usage differs by seat type (Standard or Premium). |

### What does usage depend on?

It depends on conversation length and complexity, the features you use, the model you choose, the effort you choose, and your plan. With the same limit, long conversations or bigger models use it up faster.

### How far are limits shared?

- Claude (web and apps), Claude Code, and the desktop app **share the same limit**. Usage from Claude Code in VS Code, Cursor, or JetBrains counts toward it too.
- Team limits are **per member**, not for the whole team.
- Cloud sessions also count toward your plan usage with no separate VM charge. See [C13. Cloud sessions](/en/claude/c13-cloud-sessions/) for details.

### Absolute numbers are not published

<Aside type="note" title="How many can I use?">
Specific numbers, such as how many messages or tokens per session, are not in the official docs. The official help says only that usage depends on conversation length and complexity, the features you use, the model, and the effort level you choose, without giving numbers (confirmed 2026-10-13). Treat any number you can't confirm in official documentation as unsupported. The only thing you can be sure of is your own usage as shown in Settings > Usage. Even the plan comparison gives only **relative multipliers**, like "5x Pro" or "10x Pro."
</Aside>

### Fable model limits (as of 2026-10-31)

How you can use Fable models (see [C1](/en/claude/c01-overview/) for the model overview) depends on your plan.

- **Max, Ultra, Team Premium seats, Enterprise Premium seats**: Fable 5 and 5.1 are included in the plan, so you can use **up to 50% of your weekly limit on Fable at no extra cost**. That 50% isn't extra capacity but a cap within the same weekly limit, and Fable uses up your limit faster than other models. After that you can keep going with usage credits or switch to another model and stay within your plan limits. The pricing page also says "Fable included, up to half your weekly limit" for Max and Ultra.
- **Pro, Team Standard, Enterprise Standard seats**: Fable isn't included in the plan limit and can only be used with usage credits (pay as you go). The Pro card on the pricing page says "Fable, paid with usage credits."
- **Free**: Fable isn't available.
- The Claude Code doc for Pro, Max, and Ultra says "Max and Ultra plans also have a separate weekly limit for Fable." Read it as a Fable-specific weekly limit that is shown separately from your overall weekly limit.

What's shown can differ by account, so **check Settings > Usage on your screen.** Also, to use Fable in Claude Code you need v2.1.170 or later for Fable 5 and v2.1.255 or later for Fable 5.1. (The Claude Code Model configuration doc says Fable 5.1 needs v2.1.257 or later. As of 2026-10-12 the two docs disagreed, so if you don't see it, update to the latest version.)

### If you've seen an item called "cloud session credits"

<Aside type="caution" title="An item that may appear on some accounts">
The official docs say cloud sessions "count toward plan usage with no separate VM charge." But on some accounts, the Settings > Usage screen shows an item called **"cloud session credits,"** and the on-screen description says "applied automatically to cloud sessions; once used up or expired, your plan's regular usage applies."

**As of 2026-10-13, this item isn't explained in the official docs.** It doesn't appear in the usage limits doc, the Pro/Max doc for Claude Code, or the cloud sessions doc. So it hasn't been officially stated whether it's the same as the usage credits (extra usage) above, or who gets it under what conditions. If you see it on your screen, go by the on-screen description; if you don't see it, nothing is wrong.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Check your usage**

   Log in to claude.ai and go to Settings > Usage. You can see how much of your current session limit and weekly limit you've used, and the next reset time.

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Session and weekly limit display on the Settings > Usage screen" officialUrl="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-extra-usage-for-paid-claude-plans" />

   If you use Claude Code, you can also check in the terminal.

   ```sh
   /usage
   /status
   ```

2. **Pick the plan that fits you**

   Compare plans in the [quick reference](#quick-reference) below. A sensible order is to start with Free, look at Pro if you hit the limit often, look at Max if that's still not enough, and look at Ultra if you run long tasks all day. If several people will use it together, look at Team.

3. **Turn on extra usage (usage credits) (optional)**

   Turn it on only if you want to keep working after you hit your limit. It's available for Pro, Max, Ultra (and Team and seat-based Enterprise).

   1. Go to Settings > Usage > Usage credits and click Enable.
   2. Register a payment method.
   3. Set a **monthly spending limit**. You can also choose to have no limit, but it's safer to set a cap at first.
   4. Top up your credits in advance (Add funds).

   You can also set up auto-reload and usage alerts. If you subscribed through the mobile app, activating and buying can only be done **on the web**.

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="The screen for turning on usage credits and setting a monthly spending limit" officialUrl="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-extra-usage-for-paid-claude-plans" />

   In Claude Code, set it up with the `/usage-credits` command.

   <Aside type="tip" title="When does it kick in?">
   For Pro, it applies after you use up both the 5-hour and weekly limits. For Max, it applies after you use up the 5-hour, weekly, and Fable weekly limits. For Ultra, it applies after the weekly and Fable weekly limits (verified Ultra accounts have no 5-hour limit). Research mode and Project content also use credits once you go past your limit.
   </Aside>

4. **Know what to do when you hit a limit**

   The fix depends on the type of message.

   - **Per model family** messages like `You've hit your Opus limit` or `Sonnet limit` → In Claude Code you can keep going by switching to a different family with `/model`.
   - `session limit` or `weekly limit` messages → These are **shared across all models**, so switching models won't clear them.

   If it's a limit shared across all models, your options are:

   - Wait for the reset (check the next reset in Settings > Usage)
   - Keep going with extra usage (usage credits)
   - Upgrade one tier: Pro → Max → Ultra
   - Buy API credits in the Console and use those

5. **Build habits that save usage**

   See the [practical tips](#practical-tips) below and use `/clear`, smaller models, and lower effort where they fit.

</Steps>

### How to get your monthly API credits (Max, Ultra, Team, as of 2026-10-31)

Since 2026-10-07, Max and Team plans (and the new Ultra plan) include monthly API credits. They don't arrive automatically; you have to **link a Claude Console organization to your plan** to receive them.

1. In claude.ai, go to Settings > Billing (Max or Ultra) or Organization settings > Billing (Team).
2. In the API credits section, choose the Console organization that will receive the credits, agree to the terms, and link it. If you don't have a Console organization, you can create one first at platform.claude.com or during the linking process.
3. The credits show up in the Console under Settings > Billing > Promotional credits, with the amount and expiration date.

| Plan | Monthly credits |
| --- | --- |
| Max | $100 |
| Ultra | $200 |
| Team Standard seat | $20 per seat |
| Team Premium seat | $100 per seat |

- On Team, all seats' credits are pooled into one balance, capped at $500 per month.
- Required permissions: for Max and Ultra, the subscriber themselves; for Team, the Primary Owner or Owner; and in the Console organization, one of Owner, Admin, or Billing.
- New subscribers can receive them after 7 days. Free, Pro, and Enterprise are not eligible (official API credits doc: Max, Ultra, Team).
- One plan can be linked to only one Console organization, and you can't change it yourself (contact support). Choose the organization where you plan to use the credits.
- These credits can be used for the Claude API, Managed Agents, the Agent SDK, and the Playground, but not for Claude Code, extra usage in the apps, or AWS, Bedrock, Google Cloud, or Foundry. They refresh every billing cycle, and unused credits don't roll over. Credits don't change your Claude or Claude Code usage limits.
- If you're eligible but don't see them in settings, contact support.
- The pricing page says "Premium is 5x Standard." Since Standard is 1.25x Pro, Premium is 6.25x, so the two descriptions agree.

## Common sticking points

### I switched models but I'm still blocked

- **Cause**: `session limit` and `weekly limit` messages are shared across all models, so switching models doesn't help.
- **Fix**: Wait until the reset, turn on extra usage (usage credits), or consider upgrading. If the message points to a specific model family like `Opus limit`, switch to another family with `/model`.

### I only used Claude Code, but my limit in the app dropped too

- **Cause**: The Claude app, Claude Code, and the desktop app share the same limit. Usage in VS Code, Cursor, and JetBrains counts too.
- **Fix**: Check your total usage in Settings > Usage. Running several tasks in parallel drains it faster.

### I try to pick a Fable model and it asks for credits

- **Cause**: On Pro, Team Standard, and Enterprise Standard seats, Fable is described as not included in the plan limit and available only with usage credits (pay as you go). Max, Ultra, and Premium seats can also be asked for credits once they pass 50% of the weekly limit.
- **Fix**: Check your plan and seat type (or consider Max or Ultra), and check what's actually shown in Settings > Usage. If you're using Claude Code, check the version too (v2.1.170 or later for Fable 5, v2.1.255 or later for Fable 5.1).

### I can't turn on extra usage in the app

- **Cause**: If you subscribed through the mobile app, you can only activate and buy on the web.
- **Fix**: Do it on the web in claude.ai under Settings > Usage.

### I bought credits but the API credits included with Max or Ultra aren't going down

- **Cause**: The monthly API credits included with a subscription (Max, Ultra, Team) are separate from usage credits, and can't be used in Claude Code or the Claude apps. Usage credits are billed separately from your subscription.
- **Fix**: Manage the two separately. Credits generally don't expire. However, in Japan they are described as expiring 6 months after purchase starting 2026-09-10.

### I'm worried my teammates' heavy use drained my limit

- **Cause**: Team limits are per member, not for the whole team.
- **Fix**: Your limit goes down based on your own usage. Check your own screen in Settings > Usage.

## Practical tips

- **Use `/clear` between unrelated tasks.** That way you don't keep dragging along a long conversation history.
- **Pick a smaller model that fits the task.** There's no need to use a big model for simple jobs. See [C1. Claude at a glance](/en/claude/c01-overview/) for how the models differ.
- **Lower the effort.** For simple tasks, consider lowering the effort.
- **Parallel work uses more of your limit.** Running several cloud sessions at once or running routines often drains it faster.
- **Set a cap on credits first.** When you turn on usage credits, set a monthly spending limit and usage alerts first for peace of mind.
- **Recheck prices on the official pages.** The prices in this document are US prices as of 2026-10-31.
