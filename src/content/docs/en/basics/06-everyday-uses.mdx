- Copy a prompt and just change the `[bracketed]` parts to fit your situation.

## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Learn 10 everyday tasks that are good to hand off to AI.
- Adapt the copy-and-paste prompts to make them your own.
- Learn how to look at a result and ask for more polish.

## Concepts to know first

When you hand a task to AI, results improve if you say **what, for whom, and in what shape**. We've built these three into the prompts. You can find more detailed tips in [tutorial 3](/en/basics/03-asking-well/). And first, take a look at the "what not to enter" list in [tutorial 5 on privacy](/en/basics/05-privacy-safety/).

1. **Drafting emails and messages**

   <PromptBox>
   Write a polite draft email to send to a client contact. Content: letting them know I'll send the quote they asked for by [day of the week]. Keep it within 5 sentences, in a courteous tone.
   </PromptBox>

2. **Summarizing long text**

   Paste in an article or notice and ask. Don't upload text that contains personal information or company secrets.

   <PromptBox>
   Summarize the text below in 3 key lines that even an elementary school student could understand. At the end, tell me if there's anything I need to do.

   [Paste text here]
   </PromptBox>

3. **Translating and matching tone**

   <PromptBox>
   Translate the following sentence into natural [target language]. It's a casual message to a friend. Also show me one more formal version.

   [Sentence to translate]
   </PromptBox>

4. **Planning a trip**

   <PromptBox>
   I'm taking a [3-day, 2-night] trip to [city]. I'm traveling with [my two parents], who find it hard to walk a lot. Make a table of each day's schedule split into morning, afternoon, and evening. Mark restaurants and anything else that needs checking, so I can confirm opening hours myself.
   </PromptBox>

   Opening hours and prices can change. Be sure to check the official site before you go.

5. **Getting recipe ideas from what's in the fridge**

   <PromptBox>
   I have [eggs, green onions, tofu, kimchi] in the fridge. Recommend 3 dishes I can make in 20 minutes, and give me step-by-step instructions for one of them. Allergies: [none].
   </PromptBox>

6. **Putting a difficult document into plain language**

   <PromptBox>
   Explain the notice below in easy words, as if explaining to an older person. Mark separately any deadlines or amounts I can't afford to miss.

   [Paste notice here]
   </PromptBox>

   For documents with legal effect, use this only as a reference and confirm with the original and the agency responsible.

7. **Studying and learning a new concept**

   <PromptBox>
   Explain [topic] as if to a middle schooler. Give one analogy, one short example, and at the end 3 quiz questions to check whether I understood.
   </PromptBox>

8. **Organizing your schedule and to-dos**

   <PromptBox>
   I'll list this week's to-dos below. Sort them by urgency and by importance, and make a day-by-day plan. Don't put more than 3 things on any one day.

   [To-do list]
   </PromptBox>

9. **Getting ideas for gifts, names, and phrases**

   <PromptBox>
   Suggest 10 birthday gift ideas for [a friend in their 30s who works in an office] within a budget of [$30]. Give a one-line reason for each.
   </PromptBox>

10. **Polishing your own writing**

    <PromptBox>
    Fix the self-introduction below, focusing only on spelling and awkward phrasing. Don't change the content, and list what you changed.

    [Paste your text here]
    </PromptBox>

</Steps>

## Quick reference

| What you want to do | What to be sure to include |
| --- | --- |
| Writing | Recipient, length, tone |
| Summarizing | How many lines, for whom |
| Translating | The situation, level of formality |
| Planning | Dates, number of people, budget, things to avoid |
| Ideas | Number, budget, who it's for |

## Common sticking points

### The answer is too long / too generic

- **Cause**: You didn't say the length and mood you wanted.
- **Fix**: Keep going with requests like "in 3 lines" or "with more concrete examples."

### The places, prices, or opening hours were wrong

- **Cause**: AI can state wrong content plausibly.
- **Fix**: Check important facts on official sites. See [tutorial 4](/en/basics/04-fact-checking/) for how.

### I pasted the prompt and the `[brackets]` came through as is

- **Cause**: You sent it without swapping in your own details.
- **Fix**: Replace what's inside `[ ]` with your situation and send it again.

### I'm not sure how much I can hand off

- **Cause**: The line between risky and safe tasks is blurry.
- **Fix**: Drafts, ideas, and explanations are safe. For diagnoses, contracts, and investment decisions, use it only as a reference and confirm with an expert.

## Practical tips

- Save prompts you like in a notes app and reuse them.
- Don't expect a perfect answer in one go; **asking for revisions about three times** is typical.
- Try the same question on another service and you'll see the differences. See the [comparison tutorial](/en/chat/05-compare-assistants/).
