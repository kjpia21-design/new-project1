## Who this is for

This page suits people whose work moves between documents and people, such as general affairs, HR, finance, planning, operations, and team managers.

- After every meeting, writing up the notes and follow-up tasks becomes another job.
- Writing reports and emails takes more time than you'd expect.
- Cleaning up and summarizing Excel data is always about the same, yet it takes a lot of manual effort.

## Top 5 ways AI helps

### 1. Organize meeting notes and pull out to-dos

**What it can do**: Extract decisions, owners, and deadlines into a table from your notes or a transcript.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. For recordings, first turn them into text with [Transcribe and organize meeting and lecture recordings](/en/media/14-transcribe-recordings/). Summarizing tips are in [Summarize meeting notes and long documents and pull out to-dos](/en/chat/02-summarize-documents/).

<PromptBox label="Organize meeting notes">
Below are my meeting notes. Organize them in this format.
1) The meeting's purpose in one line
2) Decisions (numbered list)
3) Action items table: task, owner, deadline, notes
4) Open issues not yet decided
If an owner or deadline isn't in the notes, write "TBD" and don't make one up.

[Paste meeting notes]
</PromptBox>

### 2. Draft reports and plans

**What it can do**: Set up an outline, write a draft for each section, and adjust the tone for the reader.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. Follow [Write and polish report and proposal drafts](/en/chat/04-write-reports/) for the full flow.

<PromptBox label="Outline a report">
I'm going to write a report on [topic] for [audience: e.g., executives, teammates].
Purpose: [request a decision / share status / make a proposal]
Length: [number] pages
First, suggest a table of contents and a key sentence for each section. Once I confirm, we'll draft section by section. Don't include any numbers that aren't in the materials I give you.
Reference materials: [paste materials]
</PromptBox>

### 3. Summarize Excel and CSV data

**What it can do**: Upload a table and it finds totals, trends, and odd values, and can also suggest Excel formulas.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI that can analyze files. See [Let AI analyze your Excel and CSV data](/en/chat/03-data-analysis/).

<PromptBox label="Explore the data">
The attached table is data about [what the data is].
1) First check the column layout, blank cells, duplicates, and odd values.
2) Summarize [monthly / by-department] totals and changes in a table.
3) Tell me 3 notable changes and questions worth checking.
Write down how you calculated things so I can verify them.
</PromptBox>

### 4. Write emails and announcements

**What it can do**: Draft emails with a tricky tone, such as requests, apologies, reminders, and announcements.
**Suggested tools**: A chat AI. To use it inside Gmail, see [Practical use of AI features (Gemini) in Google Workspace](/en/automation/19-google-workspace-gemini/).

<PromptBox label="Email draft">
Write an email for the situation below.
Recipient: [title, relationship]
Purpose: [request / scheduling / delay notice / reminder]
Must include: [dates, what's being requested]
Tone: polite but concise, within 5 sentences. Also give me 3 subject line options.
</PromptBox>

### 5. Morning briefing and schedule planning

**What it can do**: Read your email and calendar and organize today's to-dos and the emails you need to reply to.
**Suggested tools**: Claude connectors and similar. Follow [Connect Gmail and Calendar to AI for a morning briefing](/en/automation/17-morning-briefing/) for setup.

<PromptBox label="Organize today's to-dos">
Look at today's schedule and the emails that have come in since yesterday, and organize the following.
1) The 3 things I must do today
2) Emails that need a reply, and their priority
3) What to prepare before meetings
Only read my email. Don't send or delete anything.
</PromptBox>

## What to watch out for

<Aside type="caution" title="Before you paste in internal materials">
- **Company and personal information**: Don't casually paste in performance reviews, salaries, customer lists, or contracts. Check your company's AI usage policy first, and mask names and contact details. The standards are in [Using AI while protecting personal and company information](/en/basics/05-privacy-safety/).
- **Numbers and facts**: Check any numbers AI calculates or summarizes against the original, and do the same for laws, regulations, and dates. The method is in [Can you trust AI answers?](/en/basics/04-fact-checking/).
- **Email and calendar connections**: Grant read access first, and decide carefully about send and delete permissions. See [How to use personal AI agents safely](/en/agents/05-agent-safety/).
- **Copyright**: Don't copy external reports or articles word for word, and keep track of your sources.
</Aside>
