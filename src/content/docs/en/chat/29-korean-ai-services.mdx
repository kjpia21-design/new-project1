<Summary>
- This guide is mainly for users in Korea, since these services are built around Korean apps and daily life.
- Domestic AI often **comes built into apps you already use.** Examples are AI Briefing and AI Tab in Naver Search, Kanana in KakaoTalk, and SK Telecom's A. (pronounced "A-dot").
- Services change often. Wrtn's official help center says it discontinued some features, such as image generation, on 2026-03-09 (as of 2026-10-14).
- Naver's CLOVA X chat service is described on Naver's corporate page, but we couldn't open the service site, so **we couldn't confirm whether it's currently available.**

## What you'll be able to do

- Describe in one sentence what each Korean AI service, such as Naver, Kakao, Wrtn, and A., does.
- Learn the **order for checking on official pages** whether a service has been discontinued or renamed.
- Pick the right service for everyday-life questions in Korea, like finding places, shopping, and organizing schedules.
- Compare overseas and Korean services on objective criteria (form of delivery, pricing, Korean-specific features).

## Concepts first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **HyperCLOVA X** | A large language model Naver developed in-house. It is the basis for Naver's services and its enterprise development tool (CLOVA Studio). |
| **Kanana** | The name of Kakao's AI service. "Kanana in KakaoTalk," which is used inside KakaoTalk, is confirmed on the official guide page. |
| **On-device AI** | AI that runs inside your own device, not in the cloud. Kanana says it processes the original text of conversations only on the device. |
| **Sun-talk (AI-initiated message)** | A notification message where the AI starts the conversation. Kanana's terms say sun-talk may include ads. |
| **Service vs. model** | The "service" is the app you use, and the "model" is the AI engine behind it. Even if a model has a name, there may be no app for general users. |

<Steps>

1. **Decide what you want to use it for**

   - For finding places, shopping, and recent news, AI built into search is convenient. Naver's AI Briefing and AI Tab fit here.
   - If you want help with schedules and appointments in KakaoTalk, look at Kanana in KakaoTalk.
   - For writing or free-form conversation, a conversational service like Wrtn fits.
   - For anything tied to phone calls, like call summaries and checking spam calls, look at A. Phone.

2. **Check each service's current status**

   All of the summaries below were confirmed on official pages on 2026-10-14.

   - **Naver AI Briefing**: Naver describes it as summarizing the key information that fits your search intent and linking to the creators and original content sources.
   - **Naver AI Tab**: Naver describes it as understanding complex questions in conversation, synthesizing information to answer, and going on to actions like reservations and purchases. The intro page's example is a question like "Find a dryer for a 2-person household under 1 million won (KRW)."
   - **Kakao Kanana in KakaoTalk**: It says to find and use Kanana in your KakaoTalk friends list, and if you don't see it, search for "카나나" (Kanana) in KakaoTalk search. It can be used after signing up on devices that support the On-Device AI feature; see the official page's list for supported devices (iPhone 14 Pro and above, Galaxy S22 and above, and so on).
   - **Wrtn**: The help center says chat (Supporter chat) stays. The same notice says internet search, image generation within tools, and other features ended on 2026-03-09.
   - **SK Telecom A.**: SK Telecom's site presents it as "A., your own personal AI assistant." The A. Phone app (Google Play) describes call recording and summaries, AI spam blocking, interpreter calls, and more. It states that interpreter calls are limited to SK Telecom customers and are offered only in Korea.
   - **LG EXAONE**: LG AI Research introduces models such as EXAONE 4.5 and solutions such as ChatEXAONE. It leans toward professionals and businesses. The EXAONE Showroom FAQ says it is currently hard for individuals to sign up and use it (as of 2026-10-19).
   - **Upstage Solar**: Focused on enterprise products such as Solar Pro 4 and Upstage Studio, and APIs.

## Common sticking points

### I can't see Kanana in KakaoTalk

- **Cause**: It says Kanana is available only on devices that support the On-Device AI feature.
- **Fix**: Check the supported device list on the official page, and search for "카나나" (Kanana) in KakaoTalk search. If your device isn't supported, you can sign up for notifications on the guide page.

### A feature I used to use in Wrtn is gone

- **Cause**: Some features, such as internet search integration and image generation in tools, ended on 2026-03-09. It says your records and data are kept and only use is unavailable.
- **Fix**: Check the help center notice, and look for another service for any feature you need.

### A service name I saw online isn't on the official site

- **Cause**: The service may have been discontinued or renamed. Old blog posts may not match the current state.
- **Fix**: Check in the order above (official site, help center notices, app store listing), and don't use it if you can't confirm it.

### The opening hours or prices the AI gave me are different

- **Cause**: Kanana's terms also say AI answers can be wrong.
- **Fix**: Before you book or pay, check directly with the business or the official site. See [Fact-checking](/en/basics/04-fact-checking/) for how.

## Practical tips

- Starting with the AI in apps you already use every day (Naver, KakaoTalk) means little new sign-up.
- Adding "write 'couldn't confirm' for information you can't verify" to your question reduces guessing.
- In the terms, look first for **how your conversations are used** and **who owns the output.**
- Don't paste work material as-is into any service. Check your company's rules first.
- Services get overhauled often. Don't entrust everything to one place; pick one alternative in advance.
