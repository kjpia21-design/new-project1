## Data and tables

### Check the CSV first

<PromptBox>
The attached CSV is sales by branch for the first half of 2026. The columns are date, branch, product category, quantity, and sales amount. For now, do only the following. Don't analyze yet.
1) Number of rows and the data type of each column
2) Blanks, duplicates, and mixed-format values (e.g., date formats, commas inside numbers)
3) The 5 values that look strange
</PromptBox>

Source: [Let AI analyze your Excel and CSV data](/chat/03-data-analysis/)

### Specify data cleanup rules

<PromptBox>
Handle the problems you just found like this. Remove duplicate rows, standardize dates to YYYY-MM-DD, and if the sales amount is blank, don't delete the row; mark it "missing." After processing, tell me how many rows changed, and leave the original data as it is and create a new cleaned version.
</PromptBox>

Source: [Let AI analyze your Excel and CSV data](/chat/03-data-analysis/)

### Totals by branch and month, and the biggest declines

<PromptBox>
Using the cleaned version, make a table of sales totals by branch and month, and tell me the 3 branches with the biggest drop in month-over-month change. For each result, explain in one line which columns you summed and by what criteria.
</PromptBox>

Source: [Let AI analyze your Excel and CSV data](/chat/03-data-analysis/)

### Results as an Excel file

<PromptBox>
Turn the totals by branch and month above into an Excel file (.xlsx). Put the cleaned data on sheet 1, the totals table on sheet 2, and a line chart of each branch's monthly sales trend on sheet 3.

Source: [Let AI analyze your Excel and CSV data](/chat/03-data-analysis/)

### Ask it to verify a calculation

## Automation and agents

### Create a morning briefing

<PromptBox>
Create this morning's briefing for today. Only read; don't send email, and don't edit or delete any events or emails.

1) Today's schedule: In chronological order, make a table with the title, time, attendees, and location (or video meeting link), and also tell me any gaps of 30 minutes or more between events.
2) Email: From the emails I received in the last 24 hours, pick only the ones I need to reply to. For each, give the sender, a one-line summary, why a reply is needed, and the urgency (high/medium/low). For ads and newsletters, just give me the count.
3) At the end, list the 3 things I must take care of today.
Don't guess at anything you're not sure about; write "needs confirmation."
</PromptBox>
