If you're new to AI or it still feels unfamiliar, just follow the steps below from the top, one after another. We avoid difficult jargon, so start without any pressure.

If you run into a word you don't know, you can look it up right away in the [AI glossary](/start/glossary/).

If you'd like to go further, these are good too.

- [Can I use this AI output? Copyright and ethics basics](/basics/09-ai-copyright-ethics/): Worth reading after AI First Steps, before you use AI output.
- [Build your first web app with Claude Code, no coding required](/coding/27-first-app-with-claude-code/): A hands-on tutorial where you follow along by describing what you want in plain words, even with no coding experience.
- [A tour of Korean AI services](/chat/29-korean-ai-services/): A look at AI services in Korea, such as Naver and Kakao.
