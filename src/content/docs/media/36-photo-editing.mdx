<Summary>
- **Keep the original untouched.** Work on a copy, and save each edited version under a new name.
- Both the Gemini app and ChatGPT let you **upload a photo and describe the change in words**. Ask for **one change at a time** and say what must stay the same.
- **Claude can view and analyze photos you upload, but it does not generate or edit photos** the way image tools do (per its help page, as of 2026-11-02). Use it to get a critique or to write the editing prompt.
- Only edit **your own photos or photos you have permission to use**. Do not change someone's face, body or words in a way that could mislead or hurt them.
- AI edits can change details you did not ask about. Compare the result with your original before you use it.
</Summary>

<Aside type="caution" title="Photos of people need extra care">
Editing a photo of a real person is different from editing a landscape. ChatGPT's image guide says to confirm that you have permission to use someone's likeness when depicting a real person, and the Gemini help says not to violate others' copyright or privacy rights. Never post an edited photo that makes someone appear to do or say something they did not. For how fake images are used in scams, see [Spotting AI Scams and Deepfakes](/basics/10-ai-scams-deepfakes/). This is general information, not legal advice.
</Aside>

## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Set up a safe workflow that keeps your original photo intact.
- Upload a photo to Gemini or ChatGPT and make a small, specific edit.
- Write edit requests that say what to change and what to keep.
- Check the result against the original and decide whether it is fair to use.

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Original** | The photo as it came from your camera or phone. Keep one copy that you never edit. |
| **Local edit** | A quick change to one part of an image, such as removing an object or changing one color. Gemini's help lists "local edits" as a Nano Banana 2 feature. |
| **Reference image** | A second image that guides the result, for example a style you like. |
| **Likeness** | How a real person looks. Using it requires care and permission. |

### What each tool's official page says

| | Gemini app | ChatGPT | Claude |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Edit an uploaded photo | Yes. Upload an image and ask for edits | Yes. Attach a reference image and ask to edit it | **No.** It does not generate photos or illustrations |
| Edit a specific area | Local edits | Select an area and describe the change | Not applicable |
| Age rule (personal account) | 18 or over to generate and edit images | Not covered in the page we read | Not applicable |
| Getting the result out | Download full size (2K with a Google AI plan, 1K without) or Export to Docs | Not covered in the page we read | Not applicable |
| What it can do with photos | Not applicable | Not applicable | View and analyze uploaded images |

Whether each tool keeps the original file's quality, EXIF data or resolution is **not stated** on the pages we read, so check the file you get back. Plan, usage limits and country availability also vary, so look at your own account.

## Step by step

The example is "tidying a photo of a handmade mug for a small online shop."

<Steps>

1. **Protect the original and check what is in the photo**

   Copy the file to a new folder and work on the copy. Look at the photo before you upload it: faces of other people, house numbers, license plates, documents or screens can all be in the background. Crop them out first, or choose another photo. Uploads go to the service, so see [Privacy and Safety Basics](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).

2. **Decide the goal in one sentence**

   Fix lighting, remove a distraction, change the background, or restyle the look. Claude can help here: upload the photo and ask for a critique, then use its answer to write your edit request. This is this site's suggestion; the Claude help describes viewing and analyzing images.

   <PromptBox>
   Look at the attached product photo of a ceramic mug. List 3 things that make it look less professional (lighting, background, clutter, framing). Then write one short edit request for each that I can give to an image-editing tool. Do not describe anything about people in the photo.
   </PromptBox>

   For Claude, the help page lists JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP, up to 20 files per chat and image dimensions up to 8000x8000 pixels, and suggests images of 1000x1000 pixels or larger.

3. **Upload to Gemini and make one edit**

   Gemini's help describes three ways to edit with Nano Banana 2: edit an image you generated, upload an image and ask for edits, or upload several images to make a new one. Editing is not available to users under 18.

   <FlowSteps title="Edit a photo in the Gemini app" officialUrl="https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560" steps={[
     { where: 'gemini.google.com, top left', action: 'Click Open Sidebar, then Library, and click an image. Or upload an image from your device', result: 'The image is open for chat' },
     { where: 'Under the image', action: 'Click Chat', result: 'A text box at the bottom' },
     { where: 'Text box', action: 'Enter your edit request and click Submit', result: 'The edited image' },
   ]} />

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Entering an edit request under an image in the Gemini app" officialUrl="https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560" />

   <PromptBox>
   Edit the attached photo of a ceramic mug. Change only the background to a plain light gray surface. Keep the mug, its shape, color, glaze texture, the lighting on the mug, and the framing exactly the same. Do not add text, logos or other objects.
   </PromptBox>

4. **Or use ChatGPT, including area edits**

   ChatGPT's guide says to attach a reference image and describe what should change. You can also select a specific area of an image and describe the change for that area. It advises saying exactly what should change and what must stay the same.

   <PromptBox>
   Edit the attached image. Replace only the crumpled paper on the left of the table with nothing, matching the surrounding wood surface. Preserve the mug, table, lighting, colors, crop, and every other detail exactly. Do not add text or logos.
   </PromptBox>

5. **Change the style only if you want to**

   For a different look, say so as its own request. The Gemini help suggests describing a style such as watercolor painting or cartoon illustration, and the ChatGPT guide suggests broader revisions like making it brighter, reducing saturation or simplifying the background.

   <PromptBox>
   Keep the composition and the mug exactly as they are. Make the whole image slightly brighter and reduce the color saturation a little. Do not change the shape of any object.
   </PromptBox>

6. **Compare with the original and decide how to use it**

   <Aside type="caution" title="Check what changed">
   AI editing can alter details you did not ask about, such as a pattern, a hand, or the shape of an object. Put the original and the edit side by side and zoom in. If the photo documents a real product, an event or a place, think about whether the edit would mislead a viewer.
   </Aside>

   - **Accuracy.** For product or news-like photos, do not change how the item really looks.
   - **Terms.** Gemini's help says that by generating images you agreed to Google's Terms of Service and Prohibited Use Policy, and to use your discretion before you rely on, publish or use the content. Work or school accounts may differ.
   - **Telling people.** ChatGPT's guide says credit to OpenAI is optional, though you can explain how an asset was made when that is useful. Check your platform's and organization's rules on labeling AI-edited images.
   - **Download.** In Gemini, hover over the image and click Download full size.

</Steps>

## Quick reference

### Edit wording

| Need | Wording to try |
| --- | --- |
| Remove one thing | "Remove only the ○○. Fill the area to match its surroundings. Keep everything else exactly the same" |
| Change background | "Change only the background to ○○. Keep the subject, lighting and framing the same" |
| Brighten | "Make the image slightly brighter and reduce the saturation a little. Do not change any object" |
| Restyle | "Keep the composition. Change the style to a watercolor painting" |
| Avoid additions | "Do not add text, logos or new objects" |

### Which tool for which job

| Job | Option |
| --- | --- |
| Critique a photo or draft the edit prompt | Claude (view and analyze only) |
| Edit an uploaded photo with words | Gemini app or ChatGPT |
| Edit one area | Gemini local edits or ChatGPT area selection |
| Precise crop, exact colors, print work | A photo editing app of your choice (not covered by the pages we read) |

## Common sticking points

### The edit changed things I did not ask about

- **Cause**: The request listed several changes, or did not say what to keep.
- **Fix**: Ask for one change at a time and list what must stay the same. Go back to the earlier version if needed.

### I cannot find the edit option in Gemini

- **Cause**: The feature depends on age (18 or over), your account type, the app's languages and countries, and your model setting.
- **Fix**: Check the "What you need" section of the Gemini help page for your account.

### Claude will not edit my photo

- **Cause**: Claude does not generate or edit photos.
- **Fix**: Use Claude to analyze the photo and write the prompt, then edit in Gemini or ChatGPT.

### A face or hand looks wrong

- **Cause**: AI edits do not always preserve fine details.
- **Fix**: Say "do not change the person at all", or use the original for that part. Do not publish an edit that misrepresents a person.

### The result is smaller than my original

- **Cause**: The Gemini help states download resolution depends on your plan (2K with a Google AI plan, 1K without). Other limits are not stated.
- **Fix**: Keep your original for anything where resolution matters.

## Practical tips

- **Name your versions.** `mug-original`, `mug-edit1`, `mug-edit2` makes it easy to go back.
- **Crop private details first.** Do not upload what you would not want a service to receive.
- **One change per request.** It is easier to see which request caused a problem.
- **Prefer your own subjects.** Landscapes, objects and your own pictures are the safest practice material.
- **Related image-making.** For creating new images rather than editing, see [Advanced image generation](/media/30-image-generation-advanced/).
