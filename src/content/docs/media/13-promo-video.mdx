<Summary>
- AI video works by making **short clips, a few seconds at a time**. The Gemini overview page says you can make 10-second videos in the Gemini app.
- The practical way to make a promo video is: **storyboard (3-4 scenes) → generate a clip for each scene → stitch them together in an editing tool and add captions and a logo**.
- Videos made in the Gemini app include an invisible **SynthID watermark**. The Gemini pages we checked don't mention a visible watermark in the Gemini app either way, so we can't confirm one (the separate Flow tool is different; see [the Google video tools guide](/media/34-ai-video-generation/)).
- For the Gemini app vs. Google Flow, credits, and watermark rules, see [Make Short AI Videos with Google's Video Tools](/media/34-ai-video-generation/).
- **Don't use people's faces, voices, or other people's brands** without their consent and permission.
- Plans, limits, and availability in Korea change often. This guide was rechecked against Google's official pages on 2026-11-01, and where we couldn't confirm something, we point you to the official page.
</Summary>

<Steps>

1. **Make a storyboard**

   Build the 15 seconds from 3 scenes. Keep in mind that one clip is around 10 seconds (per the overview page), so keep scenes short and descriptions simple. You can also ask an AI like Claude for a first draft.

   <PromptBox>
   Create a storyboard with 3 scenes for a 15-second vertical video announcing our new café menu item, the "Yuja Cream Latte." For each scene, write (1) what's on screen, (2) the camera movement, and (3) one line of caption text. Don't show any faces; focus on hands and the cup.
   </PromptBox>

2. **Generate a clip for each scene**

   Create the video in the Gemini app. The official help page says to go to gemini.google.com on a computer, open the sidebar at the top left, click **Create video** (optionally pick a template), enter a prompt, and click **Submit**. To use an image or video as a reference, upload it with Add image / Add video (1 video, up to 5 images). Video can take a few minutes to generate, and you can't use the same chat while it's being made. If you need audio, the help page says you can ask Gemini to generate it too. Choose the aspect ratio before creating (for text prompts the default is landscape; with an uploaded reference image or video it matches your upload), so pick vertical up front for Reels. **Only upload photos you have the right to use.** Make one prompt per scene. If you ask for several scenes at once, the results get messy.

   <PromptBox>
   A close-up looking slowly down from above at a cup of yuja cream latte on a wooden table. Natural light, warm tones. A bit of yuja zest drops onto the cream. No faces, text, or logos.
   </PromptBox>

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Sending a video generation request in the Gemini app and the clip being created" officialUrl="https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16126339" />

   If you don't like the result, change just one thing and ask again. According to the help page, you can keep editing a generated video with simple prompts, such as swapping objects, changing camera angles, or modifying scenes. There are usage limits, so conserve your attempts.

3. **Stitch the clips together in Canva**

   In Canva, open a new vertical (Reels/Shorts format) video design, upload the 3 clips, and arrange them in order. Canva reportedly offers automatic caption generation (Korean is on the supported list) and a feature that turns images into video. Follow the on-screen guidance for exact button locations.

4. **Add captions, a logo, and music**

   - Place captions away from the bottom 20% of the screen (the app UI can cover them).
   - Bring your logo in from the Brand Kit in [Tutorial 12](/media/12-canva-sns/).
   - For music and sound effects, check the license scope the editing tool provides.
   - If you want to add narration, see [Tutorial 15](/media/15-voice-narration/).

5. **Check**

   <Aside type="caution" title="Likeness rights and consent">
   Don't use the video if it shows employees, customers, or celebrities, uses their voices, or shows someone who resembles them. To use a real person in a video, **explain the purpose, duration, and media to them and get their written consent**. At the time of Sora, OpenAI required confirmation of the performer's consent and your upload rights when you uploaded images with people in them.
   </Aside>

   - Do the brand logos, trademarks, characters, or background music resemble anyone else's?
   - Are the facts (price, dates, claimed benefits) correct? Does the product in the video look different from the real one?
   - Have you set your company's policy on labeling AI-made videos?

6. **Export and verify**

   Before exporting, play it through once more to check the aspect ratio, length, and caption placement. Download the video via **Share > Download video**. According to Google's official guidance, every video made in the Gemini app includes an invisible SynthID, and you can upload a file to Gemini and ask whether it was made with Google AI (it only recognizes Google AI content; videos must be under 90 seconds and 100 MB, with a limit of about 10 checks per day).

</Steps>

## Common sticking points

### I don't see a video generation menu

- **Cause**: Personal accounts need a Google AI plan, and work or school accounts need the relevant Workspace license. It's also described as unavailable to users under 18.
- **Fix**: Check the availability requirements on the official subscription page and help pages.

### I hit my limit

- **Cause**: Video generation uses up a lot of your compute-based limit.
- **Fix**: Wait for the 5-hour refresh or compare higher plans. The monthly prices on the subscription page, as shown on the US site, start at $4.99 for Plus, $19.99 for Pro, and $99.99 for Ultra (5x limits; a $199.99 Ultra tier with 20x limits is also listed; check Korean pricing on the official page). On 2026-11-01 the subscription page showed the same $4.99 / $19.99 / $99.99 start prices. Polish your prompts in advance to cut down on attempts.

### The clips don't match each other's mood

- **Cause**: When you make clips separately, the color and lighting can differ.
- **Fix**: Put the same lighting and tone sentence in every prompt, and color-correct in editing. You can also reuse the same reference image.

### The Korean captions come out garbled in the video

- **Cause**: Text the AI generates inside a video isn't accurate.
- **Fix**: Remove text from the prompt and add it as Canva captions.

## Practical tips

- **Start with short videos.** Two or three clips of around 10 seconds are plenty.
- **Use just one camera movement.** Something like a "slow zoom in" is stable.
- **If you need people, use footage you shoot yourself.** Real footage with consent is the safest.
- **Save prompts that worked.** They help you keep the same tone across a series.
- **Terms differ by tool and by plan.** Read the official terms yourself before commercial use.
