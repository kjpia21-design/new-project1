## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Check whether your account, age, plan and region can use Google's video tools.

<Steps>

1. **Check access first**

   Google says a personal account needs a Google AI plan to generate videos in Gemini Apps, and a work or school account needs a qualifying Workspace license. For now the feature is not available to users under 18. Google also says Gemini Apps with a Google AI plan needs age 13 or older in most countries and 18 or older in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK. In Flow, a supported region is also required, and VPNs will not enable access in unsupported regions.

   I could not confirm from the pages I opened that your specific country or Korea is supported for every feature. Check Google's regional lists before you pay.

2. **Open the video tool and write a prompt**

   On a computer, go to gemini.google.com, open the sidebar, and choose Create video. Optionally pick a template. Describe the video in the text box. Google suggests you can brainstorm and refine the prompt in your main Gemini chat first.

   <PromptBox>
   A 10-second video of a small bakery's window at sunrise: a baker places a tray of fresh croissants on the display, steam rising, soft golden light, slow push-in camera, gentle morning ambience. Landscape format.
   </PromptBox>

   Google says text prompts default to landscape, and you can choose the aspect ratio before generating. If you upload a reference image or video, the ratio follows your upload. You can also ask Gemini to generate audio with the video.

3. **Edit with follow-up prompts**

   Google lists multi-turn editing: after the video is made, ask for changes such as removing or replacing objects and characters, changing camera angles, or modifying the scene. The overview page also describes extending a scene and swapping backgrounds or wardrobe. Keep each change small so you can see what worked.

   Uploading videos for edits is not available in the EEA, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and some US states.

4. **Download or share**

   Under the video, choose Share, then Share on YouTube or Download video.

5. **Use Flow when you need more control**

   Flow is for clips, scenes and stories, and Google recommends a desktop Chromium-based browser for the most powerful editing. You spend credits per generation. Credits are deducted when generation begins, and are returned within one hour if it fails or hits a safety filter. The model and its credit cost are shown in settings before you confirm.

6. **Know the plan limits and credits**

   Gemini app usage limits are compute-based and refresh every 5 hours until you reach a weekly limit. Google rates video generation as high usage, saying video generally uses the most. Limits are standard without an AI plan, 2x with AI Plus, 4x with AI Pro, and 5x or 20x of Pro with AI Ultra.

   In Flow, everyone gets 50 credits per day. Plus adds 200 per month, Pro 1,000, and the two Ultra tiers 10,000 and 25,000. Without a subscription, you may be unable to generate videos during peak hours (roughly 2 PM to 5 PM UTC). Figures are as of 2026-11-01 and may change.

7. **Label and verify AI videos**

   Videos from the Gemini app are embedded with SynthID. In Flow, outputs from Veo, Omni and Nano Banana include invisible SynthID watermarks, and a visible watermark can be switched with a "Visible watermarking" toggle. Google says a visible watermark is applied automatically for users in India, South Korea and Vietnam who are not on the Google AI Ultra 20x plan. You can upload a file to Gemini and ask whether it was made by Google AI. That check handles one file at a time, videos under 90 seconds.

</Steps>

## Common sticking points

### I don't see Create video

- **Cause**: No Google AI plan, an under-18 account, or a work/school account without a qualifying license.
- **Fix**: Check the "What you need" section of Google's help page.

### Flow says I can't access it though I subscribe

- **Cause**: Flow is not available in every country where Google AI Pro is sold.
- **Fix**: Check Flow's supported-region list before buying. VPNs will not help.

### The video was removed or not generated

- **Cause**: Google says it may remove videos when systems detect a possible violation of its Terms of Service or Prohibited Use Policy.
- **Fix**: Rewrite the prompt without real people, brands or harmful content.

### I ran out of usage

- **Cause**: Video uses a lot of your allowance.
- **Fix**: Wait for the refresh, plan fewer, shorter attempts, or upgrade your plan.

## Practical tips

- Plan the shot in a regular Gemini chat first so you spend your video attempts on a good prompt.
- Use one subject and one action per 10-second clip, then stitch clips together in an editor (see [tutorial 3](/media/13-promo-video/)).
- AI video can look wrong in details like hands, text and physics. Review every second before publishing.
- Tell viewers when a realistic video is AI-made. YouTube's disclosure rules are in [tutorial 10](/media/33-video-summary-shorts/).
