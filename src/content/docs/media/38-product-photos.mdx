<Summary>
- **Start from a real photo of the real product.** AI can tidy the background and lighting, but the product itself must still look like what the customer will receive.
- Gemini and ChatGPT both let you **upload a photo and describe one change in words**. Say what to change and what must stay exactly the same.
- **Claude does not generate or edit photos** (per its help page, as of 2026-11-04). It can view an uploaded photo, so use it to critique your shot and write the edit request.
- **Never make the product look better than it is.** Do not change its color, size, material, condition or add features it does not have. That misleads buyers.
- **Check your sales platform's image rules** (background, size, text, logos) on that platform's own seller help pages before you upload. This tutorial did not read any platform's rules.
</Summary>

<Aside type="caution" title="The photo is a promise to the buyer">
A buyer decides from your photo. If an AI edit changes the real color, size, texture, condition or number of items, the buyer gets something different from what they saw. Consumer-protection and platform rules can apply, and they differ by country and platform. This site did not read them, so check yours. This is general information, not legal advice. For disclosure and rights questions, see [Can You Use AI Output? Copyright and Ethics Basics](/basics/09-ai-copyright-ethics/).

## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Take a clean base photo with a phone and simple setup.
- Use Gemini or ChatGPT to tidy the background or clutter without changing the product.
- Make a supporting image (for example a lifestyle scene or a size-guide layout) and label it honestly.
- Compare every result with the real product and decide whether it is fair to publish.

This tutorial is about product photos for a shop. For general photo fixing, see [Edit Your Photos with AI](/media/36-photo-editing/). For ads and thumbnails, see [Business image generation](/media/11-business-images/).

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Base photo** | The real photo of your real product. Every AI edit starts from it. Keep an untouched copy. |
| **Main image** | The first photo a buyer sees. Platforms often have their own rules for it. Check yours. |
| **Supporting image** | Extra images such as detail shots, lifestyle scenes or size guides. |
| **Reference image** | A second image that guides style or layout, such as a background look you like. |
| **Local edit** | A quick change to one part of an image. Gemini's help lists "local edits" as a Nano Banana 2 feature. |

### What each tool's official page says

| | Gemini app | ChatGPT | Claude |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Edit an uploaded photo | Yes. Upload an image and ask for edits | Yes. Attach a reference image and ask to edit it | **No.** It does not generate photos or illustrations |
| Edit a specific area | Local edits | Select an area and describe the change | Not applicable |
| Combine images | Upload multiple images for a new one | Several reference images, identified by order | Not applicable |
| Age rule (personal account) | 18 or over to generate and edit images | Not covered in the page we read | Not applicable |
| Download | Download full size: 2K with a Google AI plan, 1K without | Not covered in the page we read | Not applicable |
| What it can do with photos | Not applicable | Not applicable | View and analyze uploaded images |

The pages we read do **not** say whether edited images keep the original resolution, whether they carry any AI label or watermark, or how a particular store treats them. Plan, usage limits and country availability also vary, so look at your own account.

<Steps>

1. **Shoot a good base photo first**

   AI edits work best when the starting photo is clear. These are this site's practical suggestions, not claims from an official page:

   - Use window light or another soft, even light. Avoid harsh flash that creates glare.
   - Put the product on a plain surface such as a sheet of paper or a cloth.
   - Fill the frame, keep the camera steady and level, and wipe the lens.
   - Take several angles: front, back, side, a close-up of details, and one next to a coin or ruler for scale.
   - Check that the color on screen matches the real item.

   Copy the files to a new folder and work on the copies. Look for faces, house numbers, parcels with addresses or screens in the background before you upload anything. Uploads go to the service, so see [Privacy and Safety Basics](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).

2. **Ask Claude to critique the shot (optional)**

   Claude cannot edit the photo, but its help page says it can view and analyze images you upload. This use is this site's suggestion.

   <PromptBox>
   Look at the attached product photo of a ceramic mug. List up to 3 things that make it look less professional (lighting, background, clutter, framing, shadows). For each, write one short edit request I can give to an image-editing tool. Each request must say what to change and what to keep exactly the same, and must not change the mug's real shape, color or glaze.
   </PromptBox>

3. **Tidy the background in Gemini**

   Gemini's help describes editing an image you upload by asking for changes in the chat. Editing is not available to users under 18.

   <FlowSteps title="Edit a photo in the Gemini app" officialUrl="https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560" steps={[
     { where: 'gemini.google.com, top left', action: 'Click Open Sidebar, then Library, and click an image. Or upload an image from your device', result: 'The image is open for chat' },
     { where: 'Under the image', action: 'Click Chat', result: 'A text box at the bottom' },
     { where: 'Text box', action: 'Enter your edit request and click Submit', result: 'The edited image' },
   ]} />

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Entering an edit request under an image in the Gemini app" officialUrl="https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560" />

   <PromptBox>
   Edit the attached photo of my handmade ceramic mug. Change only the background to a plain, light gray surface. Keep the mug exactly as it is: its shape, size, color, glaze texture, chips or marks, and the shadow under it. Do not add text, logos, props or other objects.
   </PromptBox>

4. **Or remove one distraction in ChatGPT**

   ChatGPT's guide says to attach a reference image, describe what should change and say what must stay the same. It also says you can select a specific area and describe the change for that area, and that adjusting one element at a time keeps the rest from drifting.

   <PromptBox>
   Edit the attached image. Remove only the crumpled paper on the left side of the table and fill that area to match the wooden table. Preserve the mug, table, lighting, colors, crop, and every other detail exactly. Do not add text or logos.
   </PromptBox>

5. **Make a supporting image, and keep it honest**

   ChatGPT's guide shows a prompt with two reference images, where image 1 is the product photo and image 2 is the style reference. Gemini's help says Nano Banana 2 can accept multiple reference images. A supporting scene can show how the product might be used, but it is no substitute for the real product shots.

   <PromptBox>
   Image 1 is my product photo of a ceramic mug. Image 2 is a style reference for a warm kitchen table scene. Keep the mug from image 1 exactly as it is: shape, size, color, glaze and any marks. Place it on a wooden table in soft morning window light in the style of image 2. Keep the center clear for later text. Do not add text or logos, and do not change the mug.
   </PromptBox>

   If you show a scene that is not a real photo, consider saying so in the listing, for example "styled illustration, product may differ in lighting." Whether a label is required depends on your platform and country, which we did not check.

6. **Compare with the real product before you use it**

   <Aside type="caution" title="Put the product next to the image">
   Hold the real product next to the screen. Check color, proportions, number of items, texture, marks, and parts such as handles or labels. AI edits can change details you did not ask about. If anything differs, redo the edit or use the original. Do not publish an image that shows features or quality the product does not have.
   </Aside>

   - **Rules.** Check your sales platform's seller help for main-image requirements. We did not read any, so do not assume.
   - **Terms.** Gemini's help says that by generating images you agreed to Google's Terms of Service and Prohibited Use Policy, and to use your discretion before you rely on, publish or use the content. Work or school accounts may differ. ChatGPT's guide says to use images in line with your organization's guidelines and OpenAI's usage policies, and that credit to OpenAI is optional.
   - **Likeness.** If a person appears, ChatGPT's guide says to confirm you have permission to use their likeness. Avoid people in product images unless you have that permission.
   - **Brands.** The ChatGPT guide suggests asking for an original treatment instead of imitating a specific brand, product or artist. Do not add other companies' logos.
   - **Download.** In Gemini, hover over the image and click Download full size. Keep the original and each edit under clear names.

## Quick reference

### Prompt wording

| Need | Wording to try |
| --- | --- |
| Clean background | "Change only the background to ○○. Keep the product exactly the same" |
| Remove clutter | "Remove only the ○○ and fill the area to match its surroundings. Preserve everything else" |
| Soften light | "Make the image slightly brighter. Do not change the product's color" |
| Scene image | "Image 1 is the product, image 2 is the style. Keep the product from image 1 exactly" |
| Avoid additions | "Do not add text, logos or new objects" |

### Which tool for which job

| Job | Option |
| --- | --- |
| Critique a shot and draft the edit request | Claude (view and analyze only) |
| Tidy background, remove clutter | Gemini app or ChatGPT |
| Edit one area | Gemini local edits or ChatGPT area selection |
| Exact size, cropping and color for a specific platform | The platform's own rules and a photo editing app of your choice (not covered by the pages we read) |

## Common sticking points

### The product changed color or shape

- **Cause**: The request did not say what to keep, or listed several changes.
- **Fix**: Ask for one change at a time and write "keep the product's shape, size and color exactly". If it keeps drifting, use your original photo.

### Text or logos appeared

- **Cause**: The model added them on its own.
- **Fix**: Add "do not add text, logos or other objects". Add any text later in a design tool.

### I cannot find the edit option in Gemini

- **Cause**: The feature depends on age (18 or over), account type, the app's languages and countries, and your model setting.
- **Fix**: Check the "What you need" section of the Gemini help page for your account.

### Claude will not edit my photo

- **Cause**: Claude does not generate or edit photos.
- **Fix**: Use Claude to critique and write the prompt, then edit in Gemini or ChatGPT.

### My platform rejected the image

- **Cause**: The platform has its own rules for main images.
- **Fix**: Read the rules in the platform's seller help and shoot or edit again. We did not read any platform's rules.

## Practical tips

- **Name your versions.** `mug-original`, `mug-bg-gray`, `mug-scene1` makes it easy to go back.
- **Keep the real photos.** Show the actual product in the main image and put styled scenes after it.
- **Be consistent.** Use the same background and light across a product line.
- **Do not hide flaws.** If a mug has a small mark, a close-up photo of it prevents returns and complaints.
- **Check the file you get back.** Open it at full size and look at edges and small details.
