<Summary>
- Project visibility and sharing are available on **Team and Enterprise** (as of 2026-11-02). A new project is either **Public** (everyone in your organization can view and use it) or **Private** (only invited members). You can switch later with the **Share** button.
- Two permissions: **Can view** (see contents, knowledge and instructions, and chat, but not edit) and **Can edit** (change instructions and knowledge, update member settings). Enterprise can also share with a **group** (beta).
- **Chats are private by default**, even inside a shared project. Sharing a chat creates a **snapshot** link; messages sent afterward stay private unless you update or re-share.
- Attached files are not included in a shared chat snapshot, and raw MCP tool-call data stays hidden.
- Owners can turn project sharing (and public projects) off in Organization settings. Existing shares stay, but public projects become private.
</Summary>

This guide is about *sharing*. For creating a project and writing knowledge and instructions, see [C5. Projects](/claude/c05-projects/).

## What you'll be able to do

- Choose between a public and a private project, and change it later.
- Invite people (or a group) with the right permission, and remove access.
- Share a single chat as a snapshot, update it, and unshare it.
- Know what stays private, and what to do when the Share menu says sharing is off.

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Public project** | Everyone in your organization can find it in the Organization tab of Projects, view it and start chats in it. |
| **Private project** | Only invited members can view and use it. By default only you. |
| **Can view / Can edit** | View: see contents, knowledge and instructions, and chat. Edit: also modify instructions and knowledge and update member settings. |
| **Group sharing** | Share with an Enterprise group instead of individuals. Beta. Access follows group membership. |
| **Chat snapshot** | A copy of a chat as of the moment you shared it, opened through a link. |

<Aside type="caution" title="What members can see">
Per the official help, a public project's chats stay private unless you share them. But everything else in the project (knowledge, instructions) is visible to people who can view it. In a private project, chats, project knowledge and artifacts are inaccessible to others unless they are invited. Check what is in the project before widening access.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Choose visibility**

   When you create a project on Team or Enterprise you pick Public or Private. To change it later, open the project and click **Share** to the right of the project name. Under General access, change **Everyone at [your organization]** to **Only people invited** (or the reverse).

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="General access in the project's Share window" officialUrl="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9519189-manage-project-visibility-and-sharing" />

2. **Invite people with a permission**

   Click **Share**, add people by name or email address, pick **Can view** or **Can edit**, and share. You can paste a list of email addresses to add several people at once. The project and its knowledge base are shared; your chats remain private. Invitees get an email, and the project appears in their **Shared with you** tab.

3. **(Enterprise, beta) Share with a group**

   <FlowSteps title="Share a project with a group" officialUrl="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9519189-manage-project-visibility-and-sharing" steps={[
     { where: 'Project page', action: 'Click Share next to the project name' },
     { where: 'Add people or groups', action: 'Type the group name and select it from the results (marked with a group icon and "Group")' },
     { where: 'Permission', action: 'Choose Can view or Can edit, then click Share', result: 'Access follows group membership; changes can take up to five minutes' },
   ]} />

   An admin must first turn on **Share resources with this group** in the group's visibility settings. A newly visible group can take a few minutes to appear. Members who leave the group lose access unless they have it another way.

4. **Remove access**

   A member with **Can edit** opens **Share**, clicks the role to the right of a name, and selects **Remove access**.

5. **Share a chat**

   Click **Share** in the upper right of the chat, then **Share** again in the pop-up to create a link. On Team and Enterprise, chats can be shared only with members of the same organization, not publicly. Messages sent after sharing stay private. The project article says to use **Update** next to "New messages since last shared"; the chat article says unsharing and sharing again refreshes the snapshot. Use whichever your Share menu shows.

   <PromptBox label="Before you share a chat, ask Claude to check it">
   Read this conversation as if a colleague were seeing it for the first time. List anything that looks like personal data, customer names, credentials, or confidential figures, so I can decide whether to share it. Don't rewrite anything.
   </PromptBox>

6. **Unshare a chat**

   Open **Share**, use the visibility dropdown, and change the chat to **Private** (the chat article says "from Public to Private"; the project article says "from Shared to Private"). This disables the direct link.

</Steps>

## What stays private

| Item | Behavior (per official help) |
| --- | --- |
| Your chats inside a shared project | Private unless you share them |
| Attached files in a shared chat | Not included in the snapshot |
| MCP tool-call data in a shared chat | Raw data hidden; only the conversation and final output are visible |
| Chats with synced connector content | Cannot be shared (see [C5](/claude/c05-projects/) and [C7](/claude/c07-connectors/)) |
| Drive files in projects | The Drive option is for private projects only (see [C23](/claude/c23-google-workspace/)) |

## When sharing is off

Owners and Primary Owners can turn sharing off in Organization settings > Data and privacy > Sharing. There are two settings: **Share projects**, and **Public projects** (a sub-setting; turning off the first turns off both). On Enterprise, **Share projects** can also be turned off per role under Organization settings > Roles.

When project sharing is off:

- You can still open **Share** but see "Project sharing is turned off by your administrator" and can't add users or groups. You can still change or remove existing access.
- Projects already shared stay shared; existing public projects become private and stay private if sharing is turned back on.
- Turning off only Public projects keeps sharing with specific people and groups.

## Quick reference

| I want to... | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Make a project org-wide | Share > General access > Everyone at [organization] |
| Make it invite-only | Share > General access > Only people invited |
| Give read-and-chat access | Add person > Can view |
| Let someone edit | Add person > Can edit |
| Add many people | Paste a list of emails in Add people or groups |
| Share a chat | Chat Share > Share |
| Review shared chats (Free, Pro, Max, Ultra) | Settings > Privacy > Shared chats > Manage |

The shared-chat log in Settings > Privacy is described for individual plans (Free, Pro, Max, Ultra); we did not find it described for Team or Enterprise.

## Common sticking points

### "Project sharing is turned off by your administrator"

- **Cause**: An Owner turned off Share projects, or it is off for your role.
- **Fix**: Ask your admin. Existing access can still be changed or removed.

### A colleague can't find the project

- **Cause**: It is private, or a newly visible group can take a few minutes to appear; access changes can take up to five minutes.
- **Fix**: Invite them directly, wait a few minutes, and have them check the **Shared with you** tab.

### A public project became private

- **Cause**: Sharing or Public projects was turned off. Those who only had access because it was public lost it.
- **Fix**: Invite people individually, or ask the admin about turning Public projects back on. Projects that became private stay private.

### The shared chat doesn't show my newer messages

- **Cause**: A snapshot covers only messages sent before sharing.
- **Fix**: Update the snapshot or unshare and share again.

### The file I attached is missing in the shared chat

- **Cause**: Attached files are not included in snapshots.
- **Fix**: Add the file to the project's knowledge (if appropriate) or send it separately.

## Practical tips

- Start with **Private** and **Can view**; widen only when needed.
- Keep one project per audience, so knowledge for one team isn't visible to another.
- Never put credentials, customer personal data or HR files in a project you plan to share. See [Using AI while keeping personal and company information safe](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).
- Prefer chat snapshots for one-off results; prefer a project when people need the same background repeatedly.
- Tell your team before turning sharing off, because public projects become private.
