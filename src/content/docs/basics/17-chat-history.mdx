<Summary>
- Past chats are easy to lose. **Give important ones a clear name**, and when you need one back, search for it.
- **Claude** can search your past chats if you ask (paid plans). You can rename, delete, and export (Settings > Privacy > Export data, on the web or desktop app). **Gemini** keeps chats in Gemini Apps Activity, which you can review and delete, and you can download them with Google Takeout.
- **Export is a copy, not a deletion.** Deleting is a separate step.
- I could **not confirm** ChatGPT's search, delete, and export steps in official pages I can open. Check Settings > Data controls in the app.
- Exported files contain your personal chats. Store them somewhere private.
</Summary>

## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Ask Claude to find an old conversation, and know where to look in Gemini.
- Rename, delete, and download chats in Claude and Gemini.
- Decide what to keep, what to delete, and where to store an export.

<Steps>

1. **Claude: ask it to find an old chat**

   On paid plans, Claude can search your previous conversations on the web, Claude Desktop, and the mobile apps. Just ask in a new chat. It shows up as a tool call. Claude searches chats outside projects, and each project's own chats (limited to that project). Incognito chats are never pulled in.

   <PromptBox>
   Can you find our conversation about my trip budget? Tell me roughly when we talked and what we decided.
   </PromptBox>

   To turn this off, go to Settings > Your Claude (or Settings > Memory in the older layout) and switch off "Search and reference chats." Search is not available on an Enterprise plan that uses customer-managed encryption keys.

2. **Claude: rename or delete chats**

   On the web (claude.ai), open "Chats and tasks" in the left sidebar, hover over a conversation, click "⋮", and choose Rename or Delete. To delete several at once, choose "Select," tick the chats (or "Select all"), and click Delete. On iOS, touch and hold a chat. On Android, use the "⋮" menu, or the checklist icon for several chats.

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Claude: Chats and tasks > ⋮ > Rename / Delete" officialUrl="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8230524-how-can-i-delete-or-rename-a-conversation" officialLabel="See it in the Claude Help Center" />

   A deleted conversation disappears from your history immediately and is deleted from Anthropic's back-end systems within 30 days. Your projects and account information stay until you delete them.

3. **Claude: export your data**

   Go to Settings > Privacy and click "Export data" on the web app or Claude Desktop. You cannot start an export from the iOS or Android app. A download link is emailed to your account address and expires 24 hours after delivery. You can request a new one. Exported data cannot be imported into another personal Claude account.

   On Team or Enterprise, members can export their own chats only if an owner turned on "Allow members to export their own data." The export does not include the contents of files you uploaded.

4. **Gemini: find and delete activity**

   On a computer, open gemini.google.com and choose Settings & help > Activity (or go to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini). You can delete all activity, the last hour or day, a custom range, a specific day, or one item. Deleting a conversation with a Gem does not delete the Gem, and deleting a shared chat does not delete its public link.

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Gemini: Settings & help > Activity" officialUrl="https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892" officialLabel="See it in Google's help" />

   Auto-delete is 18 months by default. You can choose 3 or 36 months, or "Don't auto-delete activity." On a work or school account, your administrator controls these settings.

5. **Gemini: download your chats**

   Go to takeout.google.com, deselect everything, then tick "My Activity" and choose "Gemini Apps" (tick "Gemini" too if you want your Gems). Choose a delivery method (email link, Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or Box), export once or on a schedule, and a file type (.zip or .tgz). It can take a few hours to a few days. The email link is active for 7 days. Downloading does not delete anything from Google.

6. **ChatGPT: check the app yourself**

   I could not confirm the steps in official pages. Open Settings > Data controls in the app, and read each option on screen. The docs I could open do list a "New temporary chat" shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+N on Windows, Command+Shift+N on Mac) for one-off chats.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Keeping a good history is mostly a naming habit. Rename a chat as soon as it becomes useful, for example "2026 trip budget - final." This is a site tip, not an official recommendation.
</Aside>

## Common sticking points

### I don't see "Export data"

- **Cause**: You may be on the mobile app, or on a Team or Enterprise plan where an owner hasn't turned it on. Primary Owners export from Organization settings > Data and privacy instead.
- **Fix**: Use the web app or Claude Desktop. On a work plan, ask an owner.

### Claude can't find the chat

- **Cause**: Past-chat search is for paid plans, and it can't see incognito chats. Project chats are only searched within that project.
- **Fix**: Open the project and ask there, or look in "Chats and tasks."

### I deleted a chat but want it back

- **Cause**: Deletion removes it from your history right away.
- **Fix**: There is no undo in the official pages I read. Export first if you may want a copy.

### My export link expired

- **Cause**: Claude's link expires 24 hours after delivery. Google's lasts 7 days.
- **Fix**: Request the export again.

## Practical tips

- **Export before you delete.** Download first, then clean up.
- Treat an export like a document with personal data in it: keep it off shared drives, and see [tutorial 5](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).
- Do a short cleanup now and then. Your history also affects what memory features can recall (see [C9](/claude/c09-memory-settings/)).
- Pages and menu names change. This page is accurate as of 2026-11-02.
