<Summary>
- All three services have a **free plan**, and all three sell paid plans in the same rough price bands: a low tier (about $5 to $8 a month), a standard tier (about $17 to $20), and heavy-use tiers ($100 and up).
- **Nobody publishes a fixed number of messages for the free plan.** Claude says "limited usage every 5 hours and every week", ChatGPT says "unlimited text chats" subject to abuse guardrails with other features limited, and Gemini says "standard limits" that refresh every 5 hours until a weekly limit.
- Every price below is **as of 2026-11-23**, in US dollars, from the official page linked in that cell. Prices and limits change often, so this page is meant to be re-checked about once a month.
- We could not confirm Korean-won prices for any of the three. See the end of the page.
</Summary>

## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Read the three services' plan names side by side without mixing up the terms.
- Know what the free plan of each service is described as including.
- Decide whether it is worth looking at a paid plan, and where to check the live price first.

<Aside type="caution" title="Prices and limits change">
Everything here is what the official pages showed on 2026-11-23. The pages themselves say prices, plans and limits can change (Anthropic: "at Anthropic's discretion"; Google: "may change without notice"; OpenAI describes its limits as estimates). ## Common sticking points

### The price I see is different from this page

- **Cause**: The pages say prices can change, differ by country, and may or may not include tax.
- **Fix**: Trust your live page. Note the date and plan name if you want to compare.

### "Unlimited" does not mean unlimited

- **Cause**: ChatGPT's "unlimited text chats" is subject to abuse guardrails and applies to everyday text chats, while uploads, images, voice and deep research are limited.
- **Fix**: Read the whole row, not just the first word.

### I cannot tell which plan includes a feature

- **Cause**: Plan names and features move between plans.
- **Fix**: Use the feature comparison table on the official page, not an old review.

## Practical tips

- **Check the official pricing page, not a review or a coupon site.** Many third-party pages are out of date.
- **Annual billing can be cheaper per month, but you pay up front.** Claude's Pro price shows both ($17 annual, $20 monthly).
- **Do not paste private data into a service you have not read the data settings for.** See [Basics 12: Privacy settings](/basics/12-privacy-settings/).
- **This page is meant to be re-checked about once a month.** Check the "last verified" date at the top.
