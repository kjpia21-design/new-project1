## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Sign up for and log in to one of Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.
- Send a first question and get an answer.
- Know what to check when sign-up gets stuck.

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Your own ID for logging in to a service. You create it with an email address or a Google account. |
| **Login** | Getting into your account. With the same account, you can continue your conversations on both computer and phone. |
| **Verification** | The step that confirms it's really you. Some services use an email link or a text-message code. |

## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>

1. **Pick a service and go to its official address**

   - Claude: type `claude.ai` into your browser's address bar
   - Gemini: type `gemini.google.com` into your browser's address bar
   - ChatGPT: the ChatGPT page on OpenAI's official site

   To avoid being fooled by ads or look-alike sites, **type the address directly** into the address bar or use an official link.

2. **Sign up**

   Click the Sign up or Continue button on the screen and follow the prompts.

   - If you have a Google account, there may be an option like "Continue with Google." That's the simplest.
   - If you sign up with email, you'll get a verification email or code. Enter it.
   - You must be 18 or older to use Claude.

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder
     caption="The sign-up or login screen (it looks different for each service)"
     officialUrl="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8114491-get-started-with-claude"
     officialLabel="See Get started with Claude in the Anthropic Help Center"
   />

3. **Confirm you're logged in**

   When sign-up is done, the chat screen opens. If you see an input box for typing questions at the bottom or center of the screen, you're in.

4. **Send your first question**

   Type your question into the input box and press the send button (or Enter).

   <PromptBox>
   Hi! I'm new to AI. Tell me five things you can do, explained so a beginner can understand.
   </PromptBox>

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder
     caption="Type your question in the input box and send it, and the answer appears below"
     officialUrl="https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/use-chatgpt"
     officialLabel="See how to start a conversation in the OpenAI docs"

5. **Ask a follow-up**

   Read the answer and ask right away about anything you don't understand.

   <PromptBox>
   Explain the second item with an example I could use in my daily life. I'm [your job or situation].
   </PromptBox>

6. **When you want to stop**

   You can just close your browser. Next time, go to the same address and log in, and you'll see your earlier conversations again. We cover installing the phone app in [Using AI on your phone](/basics/07-ai-on-phone/).

</Steps>

## Common sticking points

### The verification text or email never arrives

- **Cause**: The email may have gone to your spam folder, you may have entered the wrong address or number, or it may just be delayed.
- **Fix**: Check your spam folder, double-check the address, wait a few minutes, and click resend.

### It says my country or age isn't allowed

- **Cause**: Claude is only available to people 18 or older in supported regions. Each service has its own requirements.
- **Fix**: Check the notice on the sign-up screen and the official help pages. Don't use workarounds.

### I logged in but can't see the input box

- **Cause**: An intro screen may be showing first, or the page may not have finished loading.
- **Fix**: Click "Next" or close on the intro window, and if that doesn't work, refresh the page.

### I don't remember which account I signed up with

- **Cause**: "Continue with Google" and email sign-up can end up as two different accounts.
- **Fix**: Try to recall which method you used and try again. There's also a forgot-password feature.

### No answer comes back

- **Cause**: Your internet connection may be unstable, or you may have hit a usage limit.
- **Fix**: Check your connection and send it again in a moment.

## Practical tips

- **Get comfortable with one first.** There's no need to sign up for all three at once.
- **Use a different password from your other sites.** It's for your safety.
- **Before signing up with a work email,** check your company's policy. See [Using AI while protecting personal and company information](/basics/05-privacy-safety/) for details.
