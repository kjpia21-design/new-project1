This section is for people who have barely used AI. If you can use a computer and a phone at a basic level, you're ready. We avoid hard jargon, and when we do use it, we explain it right away. If you run into a term you don't know, look it up in the [AI glossary](/start/glossary/).

## Who this is for

- "I've heard of AI, but I don't know where to start."
- People who signed up but have no idea what to ask
- People who worry about whether it's okay to put personal or company information into AI

## Suggested reading order

1. **Your first 30 minutes**: Get a quick tour of what AI is and what you can try right now.
2. **From sign-up to your first question**: Sign up for one of Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini and ask your first question.
3. **How to ask well**: Remember just this, and your answers get much better.
4. **How to fact-check**: Build the habit of checking AI answers instead of trusting them blindly.
5. **Protecting personal and company information**: Learn what not to enter and which safety settings to use.
6. **10 everyday uses**: A collection of examples you can try today.
7. **Using AI on your phone**: Install the app and try voice conversations.
8. **Free vs. paid**: See how far the free plans go and when to think about a paid plan.
9. **Copyright and ethics**: Learn what to check before using AI-made text and images.
10. **AI scams and deepfakes**: Learn how to avoid voice-clone phishing and fake videos.
11. **Which AI should you use?**: Start from what you want to do and pick a tool.

We recommend reading in numbered order. If you've already signed up, it's fine to start at number 3.
