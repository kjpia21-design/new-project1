## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Pick a free tool for a job (chat, images, coding, translation, voice).
- Read the limits that matter before you start.
- Know which claims were confirmed on an official page and which were not.

This page is a dated map. For how limits and resets work, and how to stretch them, see [Getting the most from free AI](/basics/16-free-ai-tips/). For when paying makes sense, see [Free vs. paid plans](/basics/08-free-vs-paid/). For Korean services in depth, see [A tour of Korean AI services](/chat/29-korean-ai-services/).

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Free tier** | A $0 plan with reduced limits or fewer features than paid plans. |
| **Usage limit** | How much you can use in a period. At the limit you wait, or upgrade. |
| **Training on your chats** | Whether a provider may use your conversations to improve its models. The setting varies by service and plan. |

## Common sticking points

### The free plan stopped working mid-task

- **Cause**: You reached a usage limit, or a heavy feature was unavailable at a busy time.
- **Fix**: Wait for the reset, start a new chat, or use a second free tool. See [Getting the most from free AI](/basics/16-free-ai-tips/).

### A feature on this page is missing for me

- **Cause**: Plans, regions, devices and rollouts differ. For example, Kanana lists supported devices, and Gemini's rules vary by account.
- **Fix**: Check the official page for your account and device.

### I do not know what happens to my chats

- **Cause**: Data and training settings differ per service and plan.
- **Fix**: Open the privacy settings of the service before use. See [Privacy settings](/basics/12-privacy-settings/) and [Privacy and safety](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).

## Practical tips

- Keep sensitive data (IDs, health, company secrets) out of any free personal account.
- Check these pages again about once a month. Free tiers change.
- This page is general guidance, not an endorsement or a price guarantee.
