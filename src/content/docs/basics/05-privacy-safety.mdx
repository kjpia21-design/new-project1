<Summary>
- Whatever you type into an AI chat is **sent to the service company's servers**. Don't enter information you wouldn't want others to see in the first place.
- **Don't enter**: national ID numbers (in Korea, the resident registration number), bank account and card numbers, passwords, customers' personal information, company secrets, or the full text of contracts.
- Each service has a setting for "whether my conversations can be used to improve the AI." Checking it once gives you peace of mind.
- If you must ask about something sensitive, **mask and swap out the details** first.
- When using AI for company work, **company policy comes first**.
</Summary>

## What you'll be able to do after this tutorial

- Tell apart what you can and can't enter into AI.
- Know where the conversation-saving and training settings are in Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.
- Mask sensitive details so you can ask safely.

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Plain meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Training (model improvement)** | Your conversations being used to make the AI smarter. The default differs by service and account type. |
| **Chat history** | Conversations saved to your account that you can look at again later. |
| **Temporary / incognito chat** | A throwaway conversation designed not to keep a record or not to be used for training. |
| **Masking** | Hiding names and numbers, like "Kim OO" or "010-XXXX-XXXX." |

The three services' settings differ as follows. (Checked 2026-10-11; may differ by account type and region.)

| Service | Default behavior (personal account) | Where to turn it off | How to avoid leaving a record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Claude** | Your conversations are used to improve Claude only if you've allowed it in the "Model Improvement" setting. The pricing page marks Free/Pro/Max as "Opt-out." | Privacy settings under Settings (claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls) | Incognito chat. It isn't used for model improvement. |
| **ChatGPT** | We couldn't find the personal-account default in the official docs. You'll need to check it yourself. | Settings > Data controls | There's a Temporary chat feature. |
| **Gemini** | When Keep Activity is on, some conversations are reviewed by people and used to improve the service. Default retention is 18 months. | Turn off Keep Activity in Gemini Apps Activity, and change the auto-delete period | Temporary chats aren't used for AI training and are kept for 72 hours. |

<Aside type="caution" title="What we couldn't confirm">
We couldn't find in official documentation the training default for personal ChatGPT accounts or the details of how temporary chats are handled. Please read the description on ChatGPT's Data controls screen yourself. Also, for Gemini, even with Keep Activity off, conversations are still used to generate responses and protect the service, and conversations reviewed by people may be kept for up to 3 years even if you delete them.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Remember what not to enter**

   - National ID numbers (in Korea, the resident registration number), passport and driver's license numbers
   - Bank account numbers, card numbers, security cards and OTPs
   - Passwords and verification codes
   - **Other people's personal information**, such as customer names, contact details, and order history
   - Internal company materials: unreleased sales figures, full contract text, source code, HR information
   - Sensitive health information such as medical records

   Claude's official guidance also says to be careful with financial information, health records, passwords, and confidential documents.

2. **Open the settings screen of the service you use**

   In Claude, you can turn model improvement on or off in the privacy menu under Settings. In ChatGPT, use Data controls in Settings; in Gemini, use Gemini Apps Activity.

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Turning model improvement on or off in Claude's privacy settings" officialUrl="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325621-i-would-like-to-input-sensitive-data-into-my-chats-with-claude-who-can-view-my-conversations" officialLabel="See it in the Claude Help Center" />

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Adjusting Keep Activity and the auto-delete period in Gemini Apps Activity" officialUrl="https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961" officialLabel="See it in Google Help" />

3. **If you don't want a record, use a temporary chat**

   In Claude, start an incognito chat with the ghost icon at the top right of a new chat. It isn't saved to your history or memory. Gemini and ChatGPT also have temporary chats. Button locations can change with screen updates, so check each service's help pages.

4. **Mask sensitive details when you ask**

   <PromptBox>
   The text below is an example with the real details hidden. Write a polite reply I could send in this situation.
   Situation: Mr. Kim OO, the contact at client company A, asked to postpone payment by two weeks. The amount is [amount]. On our side, we can do one week at most.
   </PromptBox>

   Swap names, amounts, account numbers, and addresses for `[ ]` placeholders or pseudonyms when you ask, then fill in the real details on your own computer after you get the answer.

5. **For company work, check the policy first**

   Companies may have rules like "no external AI services" or "only approved tools." If you don't know the policy, ask your security officer or team lead first. Paid team and enterprise plans have separate data policies. For example, Claude's pricing page lists Team/Enterprise as "no training by default," and ChatGPT's pricing docs list Business as "no training on business data (default)" (checked 2026-10-11). Even so, follow your company's guidelines first.

6. **Know about features that accumulate conversations**

   Claude's memory saves what it learns from your conversations and uses it in later ones. It's described as not saving ID numbers or financial account numbers. You can view and delete what's saved under Settings > Your Claude (the older screen was called Memory, as of 2026-10-26). You can also turn memory off for just one conversation from the + button in a new chat. See [C9](/claude/c09-memory-settings/) for details.

</Steps>

## Quick reference

| Information | Okay to enter? | Alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| National ID, account, and card numbers | Don't enter | Describe it without them |
| Passwords and verification codes | Don't enter | Never share |
| Customer names and contact details | Don't enter | Pseudonyms or masking |
| Company secrets and full contract text | Don't enter until you've checked policy | Summarize and generalize the key points |
| General knowledge questions, public material | Fine | - |
| My own email drafts (without personal details) | Generally fine | Mask names |

## Common sticking points

### I already pasted sensitive information

- **Cause**: It happens when you paste in a hurry.
- **Fix**: Delete that conversation. Even so, you can't be sure that what's already been processed is completely gone. If it was a password, change it right away; if it was company information, tell your security officer. In Claude, memory may remain even after you delete a conversation, so delete the memory separately too.

### I can't find the training option in settings

- **Cause**: The screen differs depending on account type (personal, team, enterprise), app version, and region.
- **Fix**: Open each service's official help link and check the latest menu names. For a company account, it may be a policy set by your administrator.

### In incognito chat, it doesn't remember earlier conversations

- **Cause**: That's normal. The feature is designed not to be saved to history or memory.
- **Fix**: Restate any background you need within that conversation.

### I worry the AI's answer might mix in other people's information

- **Cause**: AI can state wrong content plausibly.
- **Fix**: See [tutorial 4](/basics/04-fact-checking/) for fact-checking.

## Practical tips

- Before pasting, ask yourself once: **"Would it be okay to show this text to other people?"**
- With a service you're using for the first time, open **the settings first** right after signing up.
- The same standard applies when uploading files. If a table has names or numbers, delete those columns before uploading.
- On a shared PC, log out after you finish.
