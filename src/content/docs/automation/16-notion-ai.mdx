<Summary>
- Notion AI is **a bundle of AI features built into Notion**. It includes Agent, Q&A, database Autofill, and AI Meeting Notes.
- When you ask a question, it answers based on **the pages you can see**, and in databases it can **fill in summaries and tags row by row**.
- As of 2026-10-10, Notion AI is included in the Business and Enterprise plans, and other plans get limited trial usage. Free and Plus plans get a limited number of trial AI responses. Check the official pages for pricing.
- By default, Notion and its AI subprocessors are said not to train models on customer data. Still, handle sensitive information with care.
</Summary>

## What you'll be able to do

- Ask for a summary of a long meeting-notes page and have it pull out **action items**.
- Search across many pages in your workspace with a question like "Where was that decision again?"
- Add an **AI Autofill property** to a work database so it fills in summaries and categories for each row.
- Know which features use which plan and credits, and follow the privacy and confidentiality precautions.

## Concepts to know first

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Notion AI** | The name for all the AI features integrated into a Notion workspace. |
| **Notion Agent** | The AI you chat with from the icon at the bottom of the screen. You can hand it jobs like reading and organizing pages. According to the official help pages, you connect apps by asking the Agent, or via **All sources → MCP servers → Add MCP server** or **Settings → Connections → Discover**. |
| **Q&A** | A feature that finds answers in the pages you have access to when you ask in natural language. |
| **Autofill** | A type of database property. AI fills in a value for each row. |
| **Basic Autofill** | For simple jobs like summaries, tags, and translation. It does not search the web. |
| **Custom Agent Autofill** | Handles web and workspace search and multi-step instructions. It uses Notion credits depending on the amount of work. |
| **Notion credits** | An add-on purchase for Business and Enterprise plans. Used for Custom Agents, Workers, premium AI models, and AI usage beyond the included allowance, and shared across the whole workspace. $10 per 1,000 credits with monthly billing, $13 per 1,000 credits with annual billing (as of 2026-10-10). |

## Step-by-step

Start with a practice page. It's safest to leave out sensitive content like real customer names or salaries.

<Steps>

1. **Create a practice meeting-notes page**

   Paste made-up meeting content into a new page. Text that mixes attendees, discussion points, and decisions works well.

2. **Get a page summary and action items**

   Open Notion Agent at the bottom of the screen (the official help describes it as "the friendly face icon at the bottom of Notion"). When you open the chat, a list of tasks suited to the current page appears (for example, create action items, make it more concise), and you can also type your own question.

   <PromptBox>
   Summarize the meeting in this page in 3 lines. Then pull out the "to-dos" as a table in the order owner, due date, and task. If the page doesn't list an owner or due date, don't make one up. Write "TBD" instead.
   </PromptBox>

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Asking Notion Agent to summarize a page" officialUrl="https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent" />

3. **Ask your workspace a question**

   According to the official Q&A guide, you click the round face icon (Notion Agent) at the bottom right of the Notion window and type your question (as of 2026-10-19). It only finds evidence in pages you have access to. Adding a time range or project name to your question makes it more accurate.

   <PromptBox>
   Find which page has the budget decision made at last month's marketing meeting and summarize it. Also tell me the names of the pages you based this on.
   </PromptBox>

   <Aside type="tip">
   Open the source pages listed in the answer and check them yourself. If several pages say the same thing, the AI may pick the older one.
   </Aside>

4. **Add AI Autofill to a database**

   Create a task-list database, click the name of the property you want to fill, and choose **AI Autofill** (or **Set up AI Autofill**). Then pick **Basic**, choose what it does (**Summary**, **Translate**, **Key info**, **Custom autofill**) and when it runs (manually, when a page is created, when a page is edited), and save ([official Autofill help](https://www.notion.com/help/autofill)). Basic Autofill is enough for summaries, tagging, and translation. If you need web or workspace search, choose **Custom Agent**, but it uses credits, so test it on a small batch first.

   <PromptBox label="Example instruction for an Autofill property">
   Read the "Content" property of this row and summarize it in one sentence. For the category, answer with only one of "Sales", "Development", "Operations", or "Other".
   </PromptBox>

   <ScreenshotPlaceholder caption="Adding an AI Autofill property to a database" officialUrl="https://www.notion.com/help/autofill" />

   <FlowSteps title="Adding AI Autofill to a database" officialUrl="https://www.notion.com/help/autofill" steps={[
     { where: 'A database property', action: 'Click the name of the property you want to fill' },
     { where: 'Property menu', action: 'Choose AI Autofill (or Set up AI Autofill)' },
     { where: 'Autofill settings', action: 'Choose Basic, then pick Summary, Translate, Key info, or Custom autofill', result: 'Set when it runs (manually, when a page is created, when a page is edited) and save' },
   ]} />

5. **Review and fix the results**

   Compare the values the AI filled in with the original. For wrong categories, spell out the criteria more specifically in the instruction and refill. Adding a default like "Otherwise, answer Other" keeps things stable.

6. **Check whether your plan covers it**

   If a feature is missing or a usage notice appears, it may be because of your plan. Check the reference table below, then confirm on the pricing page.

</Steps>

## Quick reference

### Features at a glance (as of 2026-10-10)

| Feature | What it does | Cost notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notion Agent | Reads and organizes pages, answers questions | Usage allowance included in Business and Enterprise; premium models use credits |
| Q&A | Finds answers in pages you can access | A Notion AI feature. Free and Plus get a limited number of trial responses |
| Basic Autofill | Summaries, tags, translation | Included in Business and Enterprise, no credits used |
| Custom Agent Autofill | Web and workspace search, multi-step instructions | Uses Notion credits ($10 per 1,000 credits monthly, $13 per 1,000 credits annual) |
| AI Meeting Notes | Notes based on meeting recordings | Available on Business and Enterprise; Korean is on the supported-language list |

Plan prices (monthly billing, per seat per month): Free $0, Plus $10, Business $20 (as of 2026-10-10, US pricing page). For Enterprise pricing, check the pricing page.

### Privacy (per the official security help)

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Model training | By default, Notion and its AI subprocessors do not train models on customer data |
| Data retention | For Enterprise, most models use zero data retention (ZDR); some models can't run without retention, and these are off by default and must be turned on by a workspace owner (as of 2026-10-26). For other workspaces, LLM providers retain data for 30 days or less, then delete it |
| Opt-in program | The AI LEAP program is optional: you share data in exchange for early access to new features |

## Common sticking points

### The AI menu is missing, or I'm out of usage

- **Cause**: Your plan may not be one that includes Notion AI, or you may have used up your trial usage.
- **Fix**: Ask your workspace admin to check the plan. Check the limit numbers on the official pricing page.

### Autofill fills in the wrong values

- **Cause**: The instruction is vague, or you asked Basic Autofill to do something that needs outside information. Basic does not search the web.
- **Fix**: Fix the choices and add "If unsure, TBD." If you need outside information, test Custom Agent Autofill on a small batch.

### Credits are running out fast

- **Cause**: Custom Agent Autofill uses credits based on the amount of work. Many rows add up.
- **Fix**: Test on a few rows first, and run simple jobs with Basic Autofill.

### The thing I'm looking for doesn't show up in the answer

- **Cause**: Q&A only looks at pages you have access to. It can't find content you lack permission for or that lives elsewhere.
- **Fix**: Check your permissions, and add clues like project or time period to your question.

### I'm worried about company confidentiality

- **Cause**: Every company has different rules about using AI.
- **Fix**: Check your internal security team's guidance first. The official guidance says data isn't used for training by default, but your company has to decide whether it complies with its own rules.

## Practical tips

- **Specify the format in your instructions.** Setting the output shape, like "as a table" or "in 3 lines," cuts down on rework. There are prompt tips in [How to write Claude prompts](/claude/c04-prompting/) too.
- **Ask for sources too.** Make "also tell me the names of the pages you based this on" a habit.
- **AI output is a draft.** Have a person check numbers like due dates and amounts.
- **If you want to link to tools outside Notion**, you can connect them with connectors (MCP). See [Connecting AI to your work tools with connectors (MCP)](/automation/20-connectors-mcp/).
