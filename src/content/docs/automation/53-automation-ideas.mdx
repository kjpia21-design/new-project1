<Summary>
- This page is a **menu, not a lesson**: 20 ideas for handing repeated work to AI, each linked to the guide on this site that teaches it step by step.
- Ideas are grouped by **type of work**: mail and meetings, documents, spreadsheets and data, customers and records, and connecting tools so things run on their own.
- Every row shows a **difficulty** taken from the guides it links to: **Beginner** (chat only), **Practical** (connect an account or tool), **Advanced** (set something up to run unattended).
- Nothing here is new information. Each idea only repeats what the linked guide covers, so check that guide for plans, limits and cautions.
- **AI drafts, you decide.** Keep personal and customer data out of prompts, and check numbers and facts before you use them.
</Summary>

## How to use this page

1. Find the group that matches the work you repeat most often.
2. Pick one idea marked **Beginner** or **Practical** and open its guide.
3. Do it by hand with the AI once. Only then think about making it run automatically.

Not sure which AI service to use? See [Comparing Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini by Task, and How to Choose](/chat/05-compare-assistants/). Before pasting work material anywhere, read [Using AI While Protecting Personal and Company Information](/basics/05-privacy-safety/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Check before you automate">
Features, plans and menus change often, and many of these guides depend on your plan or account type (personal, work or school). Each guide says what it checked and when. Review anything AI produces before it is sent, paid or published.
</Aside>

## Common sticking points

### The guide needs a plan or account I do not have

- **Cause**: Several guides depend on a paid plan, a work or school account, or an administrator setting.
- **Fix**: Each guide lists what it needs near the top and in its notes. Check that before you start, and see [How Far Does Free Go? Choosing a Plan](/basics/08-free-vs-paid/) if you are deciding on a plan.

### I automated it and the results are wrong

- **Cause**: Nobody checked the output, or the prompt was not tested by hand first.
- **Fix**: Go back to the manual version of the idea, fix the prompt, and keep a human review step before anything is sent or paid. See [Can You Trust AI Answers? How to Fact-Check](/basics/04-fact-checking/).
