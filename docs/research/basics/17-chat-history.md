# basics/17 chat history: find, organize, export (U2, 2026-11-02)
- Claude export: support.claude.com/en/articles/9450526 (curl 2026-11-02): Free/Pro/Max/Ultra individual users; Settings > Privacy > "Export data" on web app or Claude Desktop; NOT possible from iOS/Android; link emailed to account address, expires 24h after delivery, re-request anytime; includes conversation data + user data; Team/Enterprise: member can export own chats only if owner turns on "Allow members to export their own data"; export excludes contents of uploaded files; Primary Owners export org data from Organization settings > Data and privacy; export cannot be imported into another personal account.
- Claude delete/rename: support.claude.com/en/articles/8230524 (Aug 10, 2026): web claude.ai sidebar or "Chats and tasks" > hover > "⋮" > Rename/Delete; multi-delete via "Select" / "Select all"; iOS touch and hold; Android "⋮" menu or checklist icon for multi; deleted = removed from history immediately, back-end within 30 days; projects/account info kept until deleted.
- Claude search: support.claude.com/en/articles/11817273: search past chats = paid plans (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, Enterprise) on web/Desktop/Mobile; ask Claude naturally ("What did we discuss about X?"); appears as tool call; covers chats outside projects + each project's own chats (project searches limited to that project); off: Settings > Your Claude (or Settings > Memory in older layout) > "Search and reference chats" toggle; incognito chats not searched; not available with customer-managed keys on Enterprise.
- Gemini manage/delete: support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892: gemini.google.com > Settings & help > Activity (or myactivity.google.com/product/gemini); delete all time / last hour/day / custom range / a day / one item; auto-delete default 18 months, options 3/36 months or don't auto-delete; Keep Activity off still keeps 72h; work/school accounts controlled by admin; deleting a Gem chat does not delete the Gem; deleting a shared chat does not delete the public link.
- Gemini export: support.google.com/gemini/answer/16920332: takeout.google.com; deselect all > "Gemini" (Gems) and/or "My Activity" > All activity data included > Gemini Apps; delivery: email link (7 days), Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box; export once or recurring; .zip or .tgz; hours to days; downloading does not delete anything.
- ChatGPT: NOT confirmed. learn.chatgpt.com/llms-full.txt is Codex-focused; chat-history search/delete/export of consumer ChatGPT not in it (only temporary-chat shortcut, already used in basics/12). help.openai.com returned 403 (not bypassed).
